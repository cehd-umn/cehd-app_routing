Part of the School of Kinesiology’s mission is to conduct innovative, interdisciplinary research in physical activity, human movement, and sport. The majority of research done in the School is conducted within School labs or centers. Additional research takes place with individual School faculty.

Research within the School of Kinesiology is collaborative and cross-disciplinary in nature, but can be broadly divided into

  • 1) physical activity
  • 2) human movement
  • 3) exercise
  • 4) sport

Students interested in partaking in research while studying with the School of Kinesiology should review the labs and centers listed below, as well as the faculty bios listed on the School’s People Page.
