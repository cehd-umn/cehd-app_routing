Integrated Behavioral Health Fellowship

This fellowship provides financial support for graduate social work students who are interested in developing a career that focuses on the provision of integrated behavioral health services in a primary care setting.

This one-time grant of $8,000 is awarded during the time the student is enrolled in an SSW-approved IBH field placement during the academic year (fall and spring semesters; summer fellowships are not available).

Preference is given to students who study on a full-time basis.

Eligibility

Students must be in either the Health, Disability and Aging (HDA) or Clinical Mental Health (CMH) concentration, and must complete the requirements of the IBH Specialization curriculum (see MSW student handbook).

Students must be placed at, and accept, a concentration internship (SW 8020 or SW 8030) at an SSW-approved agency that provides integrated behavioral health services.

Application

All students who are applying for this fellowship should also complete the 2019-2020 Social Work Student Financial Aid Form.

Special requirements

Students receiving this award must complete the HDA or CMH concentration requirements as outlined in the MSW handbook, and must be placed at and accept a concentration field placement (SW 8020 – Field Practicum II or SW 8030 – Advanced Standing Field Practicum) in an agency that provides integrated behavioral health services in a primary care setting.

Examples of agencies that provide the learning experience necessary include:

  • Fairview Integrated Primary Care Clinic
  • Valhalla Place
  • Native American Community Clinic
  • Community University Health Care Center

IMPORTANT NOTE: Because this award requires an inpatient placement at an appropriate field agency, the application process does not occur until AFTER the field matching process in May.
