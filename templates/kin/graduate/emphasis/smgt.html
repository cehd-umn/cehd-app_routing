Kinesiology MS
Emphasis: Sport management

The Sport Management emphasis area concentrates on the theoretical and practical dimensions of the management of athletic events, sports teams and facilities, and the sporting process. Management areas studied include those in the public sector (interscholastic and intercollegiate sport) as well as fitness and facility management. Sport management policy and ethics are also a focus of this emphasis area and research agenda.

Students will experience a combination of theoretical instruction and practical experience to prepare them for success in the sports industry and marketplace. Students develop the tools of research and learn core concepts through an interdisciplinary curriculum with an emphasis on cultivating new ideas and improving operations in the sport industry.
