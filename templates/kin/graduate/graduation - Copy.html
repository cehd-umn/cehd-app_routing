Professor Priscilla Gibson was awarded a Fulbright to teach and conduct research at the University of Namibia in Windhoek, Namibia, from January to October 2020. As a Core Fulbright Scholar at the University of Namibia Social Work Department, Gibson will have a 50/50 research and teaching split. She will teach courses on social work practice with families and groups, and supervise students’ research projects. She will also investigate caregivers’ strengths and capacities across generations amid their burdens in the HIV and AIDS pandemic in urban areas of Windhoek to influence policy and service delivery.

Social Work Recognition Ceremony for PhD and MSW Students

The School of Social Work will host the Social Work Student Recognition Ceremony on Sunday, May 5, 2019, at 4:00 p.m. The ceremony will be held at the Ted Mann Concert Hall on the West Bank campus. Students receive 6 guest tickets for this event.

RSVP for the ceremony

Students must RSVP to attend the ceremony. If you need special accommodations or if your guests need accommodations (i.e., wheelchair access, sign-language interpreter, etc.), please let us know on your RSVP form. Please complete the RSVP by March 30, 2019.

About the recognition ceremony

MSW and PhD degree candidates who plan to graduate by December 2019 are honored at this recognition ceremony. While this is not an official University commencement event, there is a presentation of degree candidates. A light reception for degree candidates and their guests is held immediately afterward in the lobby of the Ted Mann Concert Hall. The ceremony and reception are scheduled from 4–6 p.m.

Most students wear caps, gowns, and degree hoods at the ceremony. Caps and gowns may be obtained at the University Bookstore in Coffman Memorial Union or at St. Paul Student Center, and online. For more information, please visit the Bookstore's website.
