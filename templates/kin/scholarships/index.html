Scholarships

School of Kinesiology Scholarships

The application system is now open, with a March 1, 2020 deadline

Dorothy McNeill Tucker Fellowship Fund

Award: $1000-$3000 (1-2 awards)

Funding for graduate students working with the Tucker Center for Research on Girls & Women in Sports.

Edith Mueller Endowed Fund for Graduate Education in the Tucker Center

Award: $1000-$5000 (1-3 awards)

The income from the fund will be used to support graduate education, including but not limited to graduate assistantships, research support, travel to conferences, and equipment.

Edith Mueller Memorial Fund

Award: $1000-$2000 (1-2 awards)

The Edith Mueller Memorial Award seeks to reward participation and enthusiasm of undergraduate students pursuing a program in Sport Management.

Eloise M. Jaeger Scholarship at the Tucker Center

Award: $1000-$5000 (1-3 awards)

To provide scholarships in the Tucker Center for Research on Girls & Women in Sport.

Frances Kidd Endowed Scholarship

Award: $500-$1200 (1-3 awards)

To provide scholarships to undergraduate students studying Kinesiology (formerly Physical Education students).

J Anna Norris Scholarship Fund

Award: $500-4500 (1-3 awards)

A loan, gift or scholarship for promising students with financial need engaged in professional preparation in the School of Kinesiology.

Roger W. and Ann T. Drinkwalter Fellowship for Nutrition Research

Award: $750-1200 (1-2 awards)

Fellowships in nutrition research to be awarded to graduate students pursuing research on aspects of nutrition as an important context for critical factors related to health and wellbeing.

School of Kinesiology Scholarship Fund

Award: $200-$750 (1-3 awards)

To provide scholarships to students in the School of Kinesiology.

Tucker Center Graduate Fellowship for Gender Equity in Sport

Award: $1000-$5000 (1-2 awards)

The Tucker Center Graduate Fellowship for Gender Equity in Sport provides funding for graduate fellowships in the Tucker Center for Research on Girls & Women in Sport.
