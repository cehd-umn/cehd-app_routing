David and Janie Kane Endowed Tucker Center Director Fund [23222]

Learn more about this fund

The purpose of the Fund is to support the Tucker Center for Research on Girls & Women in Sports by providing ongoing support of the Director of the Tucker Center. The Donor's wish is for this Fund to assist the Dean and the Tucker Center in its efforts to promote strong leadership and to continue the research on girls and women in sports by providing flexible funding to support the director through salary costs or augmentation as well as other activities or projects of the director.
