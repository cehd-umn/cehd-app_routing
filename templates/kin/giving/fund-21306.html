Jean K. Freeman Endowment for the Women Coaches Symposium [21306]

Learn more about this fund

To support a Women Coaches Symposium for the Tucker Center, intended to be an annual event, to provide high quality educational programming and professional development, networking, community for women in the coaching profession as established by the Tucker Center for Research on Girls & Women in Sport under the direction of the Director and Associate Director. This fund will be used first to support the Jean K. Freeman Distinguished Keynote speaker, which is to honor Jean's legacy and her strong sense of education, athlete development, community service, giving back and mentoring young coaches. The priority of this fund is to support the keynote speaker and then any remaining funds can support the symposium.
