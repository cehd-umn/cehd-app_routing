Allen W. Burton Memorial Fund [6050]

Learn more about this fund

Allen W. Burton was a professor in the School of Kinesiology who died on September 6, 2001, at 47 years of age. Dr. Burton specialized in motor development & adapted physical education. The fund is set up to remember Allen Burton and to support students in the School of Kinesiology. Funds can be used for scholarships, research expenses, travel reimbursements for professional conferences, and other such activities that support and promote student scholarship.

Potential Award: Discretionary

Deadline: Award not distributed this year.

Application criteria: Undergraduate and graduate student award.

Selection process: Selection process administered by the School.
