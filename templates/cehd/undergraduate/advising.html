Undergraduate advising

CEHD advisers meet with you regularly so you're supported from orientation through graduation. Make an appointment with your CEHD adviser to:

  • Explore potential majors and opportunities within different majors.
  • Ask questions about registration and completing your degree.
  • Find out about campus life and University resources.
  • Get support in balancing your academic, personal, and social life. CEHD advisers have put together a list of on-campus resources to support you.

Not sure who is your adviser? Log-in to MyU and click on the Academics section.

Are you a current CEHD student who is ready to declare your major? Follow these steps.

Make the most of your experience in CEHD! Our advisers and career counselors created a first-year/sophomore roadmap and a junior/senior roadmap to help you plan your college experience and prepare for your future.

How to connect with your adviser

View our guide to connecting with your adviser.

Drop-in advising
A 10-15 minute session for topics like answers to short questions, signatures on forms, time-sensitive matters, or a quick review of classes.
Email
Best for asking your adviser about general requirements, policies, forms, or resources.
Individual appointments
Typically 30 minutes and need to be scheduled at least one day ahead of time. Typical topics include exploring majors, registration planning, identifying personal goals, and support for any obstacle in your way.
