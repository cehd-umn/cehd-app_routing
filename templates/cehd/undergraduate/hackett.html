About me

I am a student services adviser for the College of Education and Human Development.

My professional experiences in higher education range from teaching college English to advising undergraduates to working in academic publishing. My mission is to make students' academic experiences accessible, manageable, meaningful, and rewarding.

I have offices on both the St. Paul and East Bank campuses so you can meet with me at whichever location best suits you. Please call CEHD Student Services at 612-625-3339 to schedule an individual appointment with me.

We are committed to providing a comfortable, inclusive, and respectful environment for all members of our communities. If there is information you feel would be helpful for us to know, including disability or other accommodation needs, I invite you to share it with me directly or let the front desk know when you schedule an appointment.

Drop-in advising

Mondays, 3:00-4:00 p.m. in 360 Education Sciences Building

Note: At times during the semester these hours do fluctuate. It is always best to call ahead to ensure the drop-in hours are still available.
