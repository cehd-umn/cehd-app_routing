About me

I did both my bachelor's and master's degrees at the U of M. My undergraduate degree is in communications and my master of arts degree is in public policy.

I’ve worked for the University as an adviser for over 28 years and I still find my advising work to be exciting. I truly enjoy working with students and assisting them in reaching their academic and personal goals.

We are committed to providing a comfortable, inclusive, and respectful environment for all members of our communities. If there is information you feel would be helpful for us to know, including disability or other accommodation needs, I invite you to share it with me directly or let the front desk know when you schedule an appointment.
