About me

Salam! My name is Eskender Yousuf. I received my M.A. in Multicultural College Teaching and Learning from the University of Minnesota. My lived experiences and personal interests have driven my professional and academic focus areas. Some of those areas include: first generation scholars, mentorship, culturally responsive practices, problem-based approach to historical inquiry, and post-secondary student development. I passionately believe in cultivating our underrepresented youth by providing them with positive guidance towards a prosperous future.

In my free time, I enjoy playing sports and staying active. I also enjoy spending time with family, reading, traveling, and any new adventures that life brings!
