About me

Tasia Tigue (she/her/hers) is a proud graduate of the University of Minnesota. Originally from Chicago, Tasia has spent much of her adult life in the Twin Cities and is lucky to have two places she calls home. Tasia has worked at the University of St. Thomas, the U's Carlson School of Management, Malcolm X College and Hennepin Technical College before returning to the U to work in CEHD.

Tasia is a lifelong sister of Sigma Lambda Gamma National Sorority. Fun facts: She has been to Coachella five times. And for the past 17 years, she has eaten Chipotle once a week.

We are committed to providing a comfortable, inclusive, and respectful environment for all members of our communities. If there is information you feel would be helpful for us to know, including disability or other accommodation needs, I invite you to share it with me directly or let the front desk know when you schedule an appointment.
