Transfer admissions

Application Deadlines

Spring semester deadline
October 1

Fall semester deadline
February 1

Application review begins after the deadline. Applicants will be notified of their admission decision four to six weeks after the application deadline.

Note: CEHD admits transfer students directly into majors. Some majors fill before others. Majors which have not filled by the deadlines listed above will continue to accept applications until Dec. 1 (spring admission) or June 1 (fall admission) or until the specified major fills.

CEHD is committed to providing a comfortable, inclusive, and respectful environment for all members of our communities. If you need accommodations, in the admission process, please contact CEHD Student Services, cehdtrfr@umn.edu or 612-625-3339.

Drop-in advising hours

Check in at 360 Education Sciences Building. Due to construction, the Education Sciences Building is accessible via one entrance. Enter via the river side (back) of the building, which is down the hill from East River Road. Access to East River Road by car is only from the south end (by Fraser and Wulling Halls). There is limited metered and handicapped parking near Burton Hall.
