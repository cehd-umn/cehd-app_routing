Transferring credits

Find out what classes to take at your current school to be on track for admission and graduation.

Note: The Minnesota Transfer Curriculum (MnTC) will meet the University's liberal education requirements, but not the writing intensive requirements. You are not required to complete the MN Transfer Curriculum in order to apply to the University of Minnesota. Consult with a counselor at your current institution with questions regarding the MnTC.

  • Community college transfer guides
  • Advanced Placement (AP), International Baccalaureate (IB) and College Level Examination Program (CLEP) courses:
  • Refer to the Office of Admissions.
  • You may explore how courses transfer to the University of Minnesota-Twin Cities by using Transferology. If this is your first time using Transferology you will be asked to create a free account.
  • Prospective and recently-admitted students: Find out how to use Transferology to determine how your completed or in-progress coursework will transfer.
  • Current students: Learn more about using Transferology to find replacement courses that fulfill degree requirements.
  • If you want to submit transfer courses that have not been evaluated in the past, visit the Office of Admissions "Transfer Credit Evaluation" webpage.
  • Office of Admissions transfer guide - use the Liberal Education Course Transfer Guides available on this page to help you choose courses that will satisfy U of M general requirements.
  • Transferring major-specific courses
  • If you have taken courses which you intend to transfer in as major courses, those will need to be evaluated after admission. Transfer course evaluation form [Word]
