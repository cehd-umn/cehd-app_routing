Connect with us

In response to the COVID-19 pandemic, the CEHD Transfer Admissions team has suspended all on-campus and in-person services for the Fall 2020 semester; however, now more than ever, we remain committed to serving students through a variety of remote options designed so we can connect with you, wherever you are. Please see below to learn more about the support services we are offering remotely.

Information sessions

All prospective transfer students are encouraged to attend a Zoom information session. Each session is led by a transfer admissions staff member, who provides an overview of the application process, tips for applying, next steps, and an opportunity to ask questions.
