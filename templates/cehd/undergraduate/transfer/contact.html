About Me

As the Prospective Transfer Student Adviser and Transfer Admissions Coordinator, it's my job to help any student interested in transferring to CEHD figure out how to do that.  I work with undergraduate students who want to transfer from colleges and universities outside the U, but also students from other colleges here at the U (CLA, CFANS, CSE, CBS, CSOM, CDes, etc.).

Questions about any of the prerequisites for admission to our majors, transferring credits, and admissions processes and deadlines? Attend one of our weekly information sessions designed to help you better understand the transfer requirements and procedures, or email me at sbeck@umn.edu.
