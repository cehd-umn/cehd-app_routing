University Honors Program

Making up roughly ten percent of the University's undergraduate population, honors students excel both in the classroom and outside of it.

The University Honors Program (UHP) serves all undergraduate honors students at the University of Minnesota Twin Cities, aiding them in their creation of an enriched, interdisciplinary educational experience.

Dean's List

Each semester, the college publishes a Dean's List, which includes students who achieve a 3.666 GPA or higher, complete at least 12 A/F credits, and receive no N grades. This achievement is noted on students' transcripts.
