About me

My name is Amani Stumme-Berry and I am currently completing a dual masters in public policy and in social work. Beyond my academic experiences, I have worked for several years in direct mental health service and as an independent communications consultant. I am driven by a need to construct my vocations around my passion of social justice.

When I am not in school or working, I am probably watching one of my favorite Minnesota sports teams, dreaming of an international trip or plotting the development of my social enterprise. I am a 2011 U of M McNair alum.
