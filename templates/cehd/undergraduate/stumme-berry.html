About me

Hello! My name is Amani Stumme-Berry. I am a graduate of the University of Minnesota with an undergraduate degree in psychology (BA) and a master’s degree in social work (MSW) and public policy (MPP). I love learning and my personal and professional interests include understanding the historical, political and social factors that have led to racial and economic inequities and sustainable solutions that can undo these factors. In my free time, I am an avid Minnesota sports fan, enjoy eating sushi, reading books, and traveling to new places.
