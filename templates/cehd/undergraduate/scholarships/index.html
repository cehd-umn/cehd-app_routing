Undergraduate costs, aid and scholarships

If you’re a future freshman or transfer student, visit the Office of Admissions for links to cost of attendance, residency and reciprocity guidelines, scholarships, and more.

One Stop Student Services has an estimated cost of attendance for undergraduate students.

Current CEHD students are encouraged to apply for CEHD scholarships. CEHD awards scholarships ranging from $1,000-$10,000 for tuition, study abroad, and unpaid internships.

Scholarships

Incoming freshmen

University-wide academic scholarships are given to incoming freshmen students and do not require a separate scholarship application. For students applying to CEHD, you will also be considered for these CEHD scholarships.

Minnesota residents

If you are a Minnesota resident and incoming freshman or transfer student, you will be automatically considered for a U Promise scholarship at the time of application.

Current CEHD students

Scholarships for current CEHD students, including

  • CEHD study abroad scholarships,
  • CEHD internship grants,
  • and scholarships for prospective teachers.

To be considered for most major specific scholarships, complete the general scholarship application.

The Department of Educational Psychology (Special Education major) has current student scholarships that are administered through the department.

The Department of Family Social Science has current student scholarships that are annually awarded and administered through the department.

The School of Kinesiology (Kinesiology, Sport Management and Recreation Administration majors) has major specific scholarships.

One Stop Student Services has additional scholarship resources.

Financial aid

Find U of M financial aid advising and information at One Stop Student Services.

International students

Find information related to cost of attendance, scholarships, and visa information on the Office of Admissions website.
