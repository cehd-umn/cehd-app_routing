CEHD current student engagement opportunities

CEHD Undergraduate Student Board

The CEHD Undergraduate Student Board promotes community building, student leadership, and interaction. Through events and community volunteering, the board supports current CEHD students in building friendships, making connections, and contributing our voices to CEHD leadership to improve the CEHD student experience.

Other engagement opportunities

Campus climate conversations
Na'im Madyun, Associate Dean for Undergraduate Programs, Diversity, and Outreach, sponsors campus climate conversations for students to talk about their experiences as a CEHD student.
CEHD Alumni Society Board Undergraduate Networking Event
The CEHD Alumni Society sponsors an annual networking event for CEHD undergraduate students to meet CEHD alumni working in a variety of professions.
CEHD Career Services
CEHD Career Services offers several events throughout the academic year, from practice interviews with employers to major-specific career events.

Additional resources

Academic and wellness support resources

There are many student support resources at the U of M to help you succeed. Student Academic Success Services provides academic skills coaching and other resources and the Multicultural Center for Academic Excellence provides tutoring and other supports. The Student Mental Health website provides links to different resources on campus related to mental health and wellness.

Campus climate

The Campus Climate website features news, events, and resources related to campus climate.

CEHD student specific resources

We send a monthly Career Services newsletter to all CEHD students with links to job and internship opportunities, events, and more. Second-year students receive a newsletter as part of the Second-Year Experience program. We also host events for CEHD commuter students.

Get involved

There are many ways to get involved on campus, from the leadership minor to student groups and research opportunities.

TRIO programs

TRIO is a set of federally funded college opportunity programs that support students from disadvantaged and underrepresented backgrounds in their pursuit of a college degree. The three TRIO programs at the U of M - TRIO Student Support Services, TRIO McNair, and TRIO Upward Bound - jointly funded by CEHD and the U.S. Department of Education.

Undergraduate advising resources

CEHD advisers meet with you regularly so you're supported from orientation through graduation.
