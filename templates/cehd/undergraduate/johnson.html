About me

My name is Kania Johnson and I am a senior studying family social science, as well as a McNair scholar. My academic and personal experiences have developed my interest in community engagement, education, and learning how to connect communities to institutions and opportunities that are not typically accessible to them. I aspire to be a bridge builder between communities and institutions, and to educate, empower, and build with communities. I'm most excited about working as a McNair staff because I'll get to make connections with people and connect students with an amazing research opportunity.

In my free time I enjoy reading, working out, and cooking. I love to try new things and spend as much time with my siblings as possible.
