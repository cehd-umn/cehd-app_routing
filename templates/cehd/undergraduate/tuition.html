Costs, aid and scholarships

The University of Minnesota offers many ways to pay for your degree. If you’re a future freshman or transfer student, visit the Office of Admissions for links to cost of attendance, residency and reciprocity guidelines, scholarships, and more.

Financial Aid

Find U of M financial aid advising and information at One Stop Student Services.

Scholarships

Incoming freshmen

University-wide academic scholarships are given to incoming freshmen students and do not require a separate scholarship application. For students applying to CEHD, you will also be considered for these CEHD scholarships.

Minnesota residents

If you are a Minnesota resident and incoming freshman or transfer student, you will be automatically considered for a U Promise scholarship at the time of application.

Current CEHD students

Scholarships for current CEHD students, including CEHD study abroad scholarships, alumni-sponsored scholarships, CEHD internship grants, and scholarships for prospective teachers.

To be considered for most major specific scholarships, complete the general scholarship application.

The School of Kinesiology (Kinesiology, Sport Management and Recreation, Park and Leisure Studies majors) has additional major specific scholarships.

One Stop Student Services has additional scholarship resources.
