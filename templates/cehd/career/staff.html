Career services staff

​​We will guide and support you! The CEHD Career Services team provides individualized career services emphasizing how your identity, values, and experiences can help you make the best decisions for your future. We empower students to recognize their strengths and potential and strive to create authentic spaces for growth, reflection, and meaningful connections.​

We also provide employers a variety of specialized recruitment options to connect with CEHD students and alumni.

We offer one hour career counseling appointments for CEHD students and alumni. Appointments are holistic, inclusive and individualized, addressing all aspects of your career journey including major and career exploration/decision-making, cover letter and resume reviews, interview preparation, internship and job search support, and graduate school preparation.

Schedule an appointment by calling 612-625-3339 or online at cehdcareer.appointments.umn.edu.
