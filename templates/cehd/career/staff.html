Career services staff

​​We prepare and support CEHD students and alumni to make well thought out decisions about their career development and job search. We provide employers a variety of recruitment options to connect with CEHD students and alumni.​​

We offer one hour individual appointments with students and alumni covering topics such as career assessment, résumé/CV review, cover letter writing, practice interviews, graduate school and job search planning.

Schedule an appointment by calling 612-625-3339 or online at cehdcareer.appointments.umn.edu.
