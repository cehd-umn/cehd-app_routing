Career Statistics for New CEHD Graduates

Survey of Spring 2015 CEHD Graduates

  • 71% reported they were in “Paid employment,” 21% reported “Attending graduate/professional school” and 4% reported “Seeking employment." 4% reported "Other" ("Other" includes military service, internship, volunteer/community service, time off to travel, family obligations, additional undergraduate coursework.)
  • 94% were satisfied overall with their experiences at the University of Minnesota
  • 86% completed at least one internship experience (this does not include student teaching)
  • 88% received academic credit for their internship
  • 32% received a job offer prior to graduation
  • 35% found employment in less than 1 month after starting their job search
  • 16% found employment 1-2 months after starting their job search
  • 30% found employment 3-4 months after starting their job search
  • 80% reported their job was related to their major

Survey of Teacher Licensure Students

Highlights from the 2015 Transition to Teaching Survey*:

    • 93.7% of students who completed the CEHD initial licensure program (ILP) report being employed as teachers in their first year after program completion.
    • 82.8% of these teachers were employed full time and 10.9% were employed part time.
    • 60.5% were employed at public schools; 11.7% were employed at public charter schools; 5.5% were employed at private schools; 4.3% were employed in other schools, e.g. international schools.

*Data obtained from the Transition to Teaching Survey 2015 through the CEHD Office of Teacher Education (Completion year 2013-2014). Response rate of survey was 91.4%.
