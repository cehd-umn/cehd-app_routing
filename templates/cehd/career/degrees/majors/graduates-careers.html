Career statistics for new CEHD graduates

Survey of Spring 2018 CEHD graduates

  • 75.1% of graduates were working part or full time post-graduation.
  • 35.3% of graduates were continuing their education.
  • 87.9% of graduates were satisfied with their primary post-graduation occupation.
