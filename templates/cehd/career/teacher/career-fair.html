2019 Minnesota Education Job Fair

Wednesday, April 10, 2019 | Minneapolis Convention Center
10:00 a.m. - 3:00 p.m. Employer Booths Open
10:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. Interviewing Area Open

Meet school district and education organization representatives, attend district presentations, and interview for jobs. The Minnesota Education Job Fair is open to students and alumni from participating Minnesota colleges and universities. Teachers must be licensed by December 2019. Education services candidates (such as school counselors, speech therapists, school social workers, or school administrators) may also attend. The registration fee is $20.

Register to attend

You have used GoldPASS

  1. Log-in to GoldPASS.
  2. Update your profile. Click on upper right hand corner arrow by your name. Click on My Profile, Select your Education Licensure area. Save.
  3. Go to the Events tab at the top of the page.
  4. Choose Fair Search.
  5. Select 2019 MN Education Job Fair.
  6. Register by following the prompts.
  7. Complete the Licensure Survey on Event Page (Required). This information will be used to create your name tag/pass to enter fair.
  8. Registration will be $20. You will be prompted to pay online during registration. Need-based fee waivers are available for students and alumni experiencing financial hardship by completing this form by Monday, April 1.
  9. You will receive a confirmation e-mail when your registration is received.

You haven't used GoldPASS

  1. If you have not used GoldPASS before, log-in under "Students and Alumni" with your x.500 and password (same as your U of M e-mail). If you are unable to log-in or do not know your x.500 and password, call 612-301-HELP for assistance.
  2. Add your profile information. Click on upper right hand corner arrow by your name. Click on My Profile. Select your Education Licensure area. Save.
  3. Go to the Events tab at the top of the page.
  4. Choose Fair Search.
  5. Select 2019 MN Education Job Fair.
  6. Register by following the prompts.
  7. Complete the Licensure Survey on Event Page (Required). This information will be used to create your name tag/pass to enter fair.
  8. Registration will be $20. You will be prompted to pay online during registration. Need-based fee waivers are available for students and alumni experiencing financial hardship by completing this form by Monday, April 1.
  9. You will receive a confirmation e-mail when your registration is received.

Prepare

  • Review the MN Education Job Fair checklist.
  • Visit the CEHD Career Services website for additional teacher job search resources.
  • Stop by during drop-in hours to have your resume reviewed in preparation for the job fair.
  • Or, schedule an appointment with a career counselor by calling 612-625-3339 or make an appointment online for assistance with your resume, school district research, and job fair strategy.

Employers attending

Each year, approximately 100 local, national, and international education employers attend the MN Education Job Fair. They represent school districts large and small and non-profit organizations who are seeking qualified teacher candidates and education service professionals (such as school counselors, speech therapists, school social workers, or school administrators).

Visit the MN Education Job Fair’s employer webpage for more information about participating school districts and education organizations that interview on-site. Check this page frequently, as the list of employers will increase as the event gets closer.

Job fair check-in

The U of M (Twin Cities)/CEHD check-in table will be open at 10:00 a.m. When you arrive, stop by the U of M table to pick up your name tag and other materials. Your name tag will act as your proof of admission. You will be required to provide evidence of eligibility by presenting your U of M student ID card or (unofficial) transcript.
