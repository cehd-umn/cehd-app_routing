Hours and Locations

Career Center hours

CEHD Career Services will continue to provide services virtually during fall semester.

In order to provide the highest quality service, students are required to meet with a career counselor through a one-on-one appointment before receiving resume revisions via e-mail. This allows staff to learn more about each student's unique background and provide individualized feedback

Location

CEHD Career Services is providing virtual services during the Fall 2020 semester.

360 Education Sciences Building
56 East River Road
Minneapolis, MN 55455

Drop-in hours

Drop-ins are available this fall semester 9/9 - 12/23 for questions about resumes and cover letters. You must be a current CEHD student or CEHD alumni up to 2 years after graduation in order to utilize drop-ins. *There will be no drop-ins 11/27 due to holiday

All drop-ins will be virtual over Zoom as follows: Mondays, 2 - 3:30 p.m., Wednesdays 11 a.m. - 12:30 p.m., Fridays, 9 - 10:30 a.m. Ready to meet with a career counselor during drop-in times? Click https://umn.zoom.us/my/cehdcareerservies to join.

Virtual Appointments

Make a virtual one-hour appointment online with a career counselor. In order to connect easily, please install the Zoom for Meetings Desktop App or the Zoom Mobile App. Detailed installation instructions can be found on OIT’s website. If you have any questions or need accommodations, please email the career counselor you've scheduled your appointment with.
