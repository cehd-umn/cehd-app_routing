Hours and Locations

Career Center hours

8:00 a.m. - 4:00 p.m., Monday – Friday

In order to provide the highest quality service, students are required to meet with a career counselor through a one-on-one appointment before receiving resume revisions via e-mail. This allows staff to learn more about each student's unique background and provide individualized feedback

Location

360 Education Sciences Building
56 East River Road
Minneapolis, MN 55455

Drop-in hours

Drop-in hours are held on Fridays from 9:00 a.m.-2:00 p.m. The last drop-in appointment will be taken at 1:45 p.m.

Drop-in hours are available to CEHD students. They are quick, 15-minute appointments to:

  • Receive feedback on your resume and/or cover letter
  • Get hands-on assistance with GoldPASS, the University of Minnesota's online job and internship database
  • Learn about CEHD Career Services resources and services

Individual appointments

Schedule an appointment with a career counselor by calling 612-625-3339 or online at cehdcareer.appointments.umn.edu. Appointments are generally scheduled between 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m.
