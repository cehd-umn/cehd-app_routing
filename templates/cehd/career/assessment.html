Career assessment tools

Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI)

The MBTI Career Report is designed to identify personality type and use that information to explore careers. It is based on the belief that individuals are most satisfied and successful in jobs that allow them to use their natural preferences. Personality type is a way of determining those preferences and which occupations may provide the greatest opportunity for expression.

How long does the survey take? Expect to spend 35 to 40 minutes answering questions.

How do I get my results?Schedule an appointment with a career counselor to discuss your results by calling 612-625-3339 or make an appointment online.

Who can take it? Currently enrolled College of Education and Human Development (CEHD) students and recent CEHD alumni (graduated 0-2 years ago). To take the assessment email stumn001@umn.edu and request a log in link.

Note

If you take the MBTI at the site above and are not a CEHD student/alumni, you will not receive your results. If you are not a CEHD student/alumni, please contact your college’s career office for career assessment options.

Strong Interest Inventory (SII)

The SII assessment is used to help you understand your work interests and to show you some kinds of work in which you might be comfortable. The SII is not a test of your abilities; it is an inventory of your interests. The report will provide information to help you understand your results.

How long does the survey take? Expect to spend 35 to 40 minutes answering questions.

How do I get my results? Schedule an appointment with a career counselor to discuss your results by calling 612-625-3339 or make an appointment online.

Who can take it? Currently enrolled College of Education and Human Development (CEHD) students and recent CEHD alumni (graduated 0-2 years ago). To take the assessment email stumn001@umn.edu and request a log in link.

Note If you take the SII at the site above and are not a CEHD student/alumni, you will not receive your results. If you are not a CEHD student/alumni, please contact your college’s career office for career assessment options.
