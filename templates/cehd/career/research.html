Choosing a Career

CEHD majors prepare you for a wide variety of careers. We encourage you to think creatively about how to connect your experience to all kinds of careers. We’re here to help you understand the changing nature of careers and work.

General tips

Use the career development cycle to guide you through the process.

Take a career assessment and meet with a career counselor.

Explore CEHD careers by degree to learn what the career options are for your CEHD degree.

Look at job descriptions on employments sites like GoldPASS and Indeed.com.

What does the future look like in your field? These resources help give you an idea:

  • iSEEK
  • The Occupational Outlook Handbook (OOH)
  • The Occupational Information Network (ONet)
  • Career One Stop
  • Goinglobal - sign into GoldPASS and click on the Goinglobal logo.
  • Vault

Talk to People

Conduct informational interviews with professionals.

Network by talking to students, advisers, faculty, and alumni about their degrees and careers.

Utilize online social networks like LinkedIn.
