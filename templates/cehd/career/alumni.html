Services for CEHD alumni

CEHD alumni are welcome to use CEHD Career Services based on the following guidelines:

Recent CEHD alumni (graduated 0-2 years ago)

All CEHD career services are available to alumni two years after graduation. These services include access to GoldPASS, the U of M's job posting site, the CEHD Career Services website, and individual appointments covering topics such as resume review, networking, interviewing skills, career exploration, and job search planning.

CEHD alumni who graduated 2+ years ago

Alumni who graduated more than two years ago are welcome to use the resources on the CEHD Career Services website and have access to GoldPASS, the U of M's job posting website. Forgot your GoldPASS password? Call 612-301-HELP for assistance.

We do not schedule appointments for alumni who graduated more than two years ago because our services are targeted to the career needs of current students and recent alumni.
