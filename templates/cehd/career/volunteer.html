More Opportunities

Employers want to hire people with diverse experiences. Volunteering, studying abroad, or service learning can all lead to potential jobs or internships.

Volunteering and Service Learning

Volunteering is an excellent way to engage in your community and gain experience in your field. The University’s Engage! search tool makes it easy to find opportunities that fit with your interests. Also check out the Community Service Learning website and stop by their office in 240 Appleby Hall.
