CEHD Career Services offers individual appointments for graduate students focused on resume/CV review, cover letter writing, and the non-academic interview and job search. Schedule an appointment online.

Career events

CEHD Career Services coordinates and collaborates on career events for graduate students which include: employer information sessions, employer recruitment events, on-campus interviews, Government and Non-Profit Job and Internship Fair, MN Education Job Fair, Networking Breakfast for Graduate Students and Postdoctoral Scholars, and career workshops for graduate students. To learn more about these opportunities, visit GoldPASS or connect with your academic department's DGS Assistant.
