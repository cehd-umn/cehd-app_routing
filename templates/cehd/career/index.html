Career Services

CEHD Career Services connects current CEHD students to their future. Career Services staff host on-campus events so you can find jobs and internships, meet with you to review your resume, help with major and career exploration, and prepare for interviews and graduate/professional school. Appointments

Make an appointment online or call 612-625-3339.

You can make an appointment for advice on exploring majors and careers, looking for a job or internship, preparing for graduate school, and reviewing your resume.

The first step in getting your resume reviewed is to schedule an in-person appointment. Drop-in hours are also available.
