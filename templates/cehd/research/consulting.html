Research and Assessment Services

Improve your grant’s competitive edge

Tight budgets and shrinking internal funding make the competition for external dollars as fierce as ever. Researchers must present strong proposals with cutting-edge methodology to rise above the crowd.

That’s where we come in. With a combination of services unique to education and human development research, our offerings range from advice on methodology, to writing method and results sections related to findings.

With specialized support for both qualitative and quantitative research methods, our work promotes both increased productivity and quality. Though we primarily serve graduate students, faculty, and researchers in CEHD, we also work with external partners.

UMN Faculty and Researchers

Funded projects

  • General advice about methodology and statistical analysis
  • Performing statistical analysis
  • Instrument development
  • Writing method and results sections relating to project findings (reports, research papers, etc)
  • Services are provided at the internal hourly rate of $$, or can be supported with the inclusion of ORAS’ services in project budget.

Projects seeking funds

  • General advice about methodology and statistical analysis
  • Writing method and results sections of grant applications and proposals
  • Pre-award services are no cost, provided that ORAS is included in the funding proposal.

Unfunded projects (CEHD only)

  • General advice about methodology and statistical analysis
  • Six 45-minute consultations are provided each academic year at no cost.

CEHD graduate students

We only consult on research related to a dissertation or master’s thesis; all ORAS recommendations should be cleared by the student’s committee before being undertaken.

  • General advice about methodology and statistical analysis
  • Four 45-minute consultations are provided each academic year at no cost.

External (non-UMN) graduate students and researchers

  • General advice about methodology and statistical analysis
  • Performing statistical analysis
  • Instrument development
  • Writing method and results sections relating to project findings (reports, research papers, etc)
  • Writing method and results sections of grant applications and proposals (Graduate students only)
  • Services provided at the external hourly rate of $$

Request our services

To optimize your consultation time, we recommending bringing specific questions and all relevant documentation (e.g. output, instruments). We do not provide tutoring for coursework or training services.
