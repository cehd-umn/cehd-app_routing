I-Connect Program

For new international students, a connection with current students can ward off isolation and loneliness, help with navigating new social and academic environments, and build lasting friendships. Current students returning from experiences abroad or seeking out more global experiences also find that connecting with international students is a great way to continue their growth and process their experiences.

We bring these groups of undergraduate students together, pairing incoming international students (I-Connects) with current CEHD students (I-Connect Friends). Through fun cultural immersion activities and meaningful engagement, the I-Connect Program is a great way to build a support network, expand your world view, and make friends from across the globe!

I-Connects (International Students)

New international undergraduate students in any of CEHD’s degree programs can apply to be an I-Connect. You will be matched with a current CEHD student who is excited to learn more about you and your culture, while sharing their interests and culture with you. They will also help you navigate your transition to the University of Minnesota and living in the Twin Cities.

After you apply and are admitted, you will:

  • Receive an email introducing you to your I-Connect Friend, and a schedule of events for the semester, including the CEHD New International Students Reception.
  • Join the I-Connect program training, and help design and participate in events with your I-Connect Friend and the larger I-Connect group

Apply to be an I-Connect!

I-Connect Friends (Current CEHD Students)

Interested in making connections with new international students? Become an I-Connect Friend! You’ll receive intercultural leadership training and support to develop your international connections and interests, and be paired with a new international student as they make their transition to life as a student in CEHD.

As an I-Connect Friend, you’ll be responsible for a monthly cultural immersion activity with your I-Connect (supported by a stipend), and to be a reliable resource for navigating the University and life in the Twin Cities.

To apply, you must:

  • Be a current CEHD undergraduate student with a 3.0 GPA or above.
  • Have a sincere interest in helping people.
  • Keep an open mind, with respect to religious, political, and cultural differences and sexual orientation issues.
  • Demonstrate an interest in developing your international competencies.
  • Be willing to commit to all required I-Connect meetings, trainings, and group events for one semester, including the CEHD New International Students Reception.

Apply to be an I-Connect Friend!
