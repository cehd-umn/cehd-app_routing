Improving lives for our children

The University of Minnesota’s College of Education and Human Development is the leader in advancing knowledge of how our children develop and learn, how we interact with our families and communities, and how we can maximize human potential.

Academic/professional program opportunites

The college is uniquely positioned to address many of our toughest challenges, especially in areas such as children’s mental health and development, family resilience, educational equity, teaching and learning innovations, health across the lifespan, and preparation of graduates who can solve intercultural and global problems. Learn more about the educational and professional opportunities that we offer.

Explore our areas of study in the field of Families, Youth, and Children.

CEHD advances research, teaching, and community engagement to increase opportunities for all individuals to have a successful start in life and to foster healthy human development, and provides programs that meet the demands of the 21st century.
