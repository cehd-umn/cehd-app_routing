Improving lives for our children

Research and teaching come together in the College of Education and Human Development. Our faculty bring new perspectives to children's mental health and family resilience. We prepare educators ready to foster equity and access. Our graduates rise to meet the challenges and triumphs of a changing world.

The 2017 Academic Ranking of World Universities listed CEHD as the top public school of education in the world.
