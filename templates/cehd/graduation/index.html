Undergraduate students

Criteria that all students must meet to be granted a bachelors degree from the University of Minnesota:

  • Grades of “C-” or better for all courses in your major, according to the departmental designation
  • Cumulative GPA of 2.00 or higher
  • Completion of all degree requirements listed on your APAS
  • Completion of a minimum of 120 credits

Applying for Graduation

Apply to graduate at least one full semester before completion of the final coursework and internship.

  1. Review your APAS report to make sure all degree requirements have been met or are in progress.
  2. Meet with both your college and department advisers the semester before you hope to graduate to make sure you’re on track.
  3. Submit the online Application for Undergraduate Degree.

M.Ed. students

M.Ed. students admitted before 2013 should follow degree completion steps from the Graduate School.

M.Ed. students admitted after 2013 final degree clearance depends on the following:

  • Transfer credits (if applicable) must be posted to transcript. Contact your adviser about transferring credits.
  • The submission of the signed Graduate Degree Plan (GDP) to your program adviser. Note: All M.Ed. GDPs are “Plan C.” M.Ed. students only need to have their faculty adviser sign the GDP form. Initial licensure students in the Curriculum and Instruction department (C&I) should have their department adviser sign the form. The DGS and College signatures are not required.
  • The completion of the Application for Degree form through MyU within the designated time frame for your intended graduation month.
  • Completion of all program requirements.
  • The posting of final grades to the academic record. (Courses may be in progress at the time you apply for your degree.)

Important: If you have a critical deadline (for example: pay or lane change awarded with conferment of degree) please carefully review the timeline below and plan accordingly. Conferment of your degree before the end of the term in any month may affect eligibility benefits (assistantships, visa requirements, student loans, housing, etc.)

Graduate students

Each program and department has specific requirements for degrees; be sure to check with your Director of Graduate Studies (DGS) and program handbook for full requirements.

All students must apply for graduation for their degree to be officially awarded and posted to their graduate transcript. Degrees are conferred once every month on the last business day. However, the degree is posted to your official University of Minnesota transcript a couple of weeks after this date. In order to qualify for the last business day conferment date, the completion of the online Application for Degree form through MyU must be submitted within the designated time frame for your intended graduation month. If the online form is submitted after the first day of the intended month of graduation, then the conferment date will roll over to the next month.

  • Masters students (including MA, MSW, and M.Ed. students admitted before Spring 2013)
