Welcome, alumni

College of Education and Human Development alumni and friends are invited to become members of the University of Minnesota Alumni Association and the CEHD Alumni Society.

The college has over 75,000 living alumni globally. With over 8,000 Alumni Association members, the CEHD Alumni Society is the second largest alumni society at the University of Minnesota. Nearly 1,200 CEHD graduates are faculty or staff members at the University. Three hundred work in CEHD.

Ways to get involved

Connect with a current student by participating in an informational interview.

These are generally 20-30 minutes in length and allow a student to gain an inside view into your particular industry area and hear your first hand experiences. Share your experience with students by participating on a career panel or mock interview.

Visit a class or student group to share information about your career area.
