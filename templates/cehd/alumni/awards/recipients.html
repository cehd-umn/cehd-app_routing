CEHD Alumni Society Award of Excellence

The Award of Excellence recognizes alumni who have demonstrated outstanding achievement and leadership in their profession, served as mentors to others in their field, or shown exceptional volunteer service. This award will be given annually by the CEHD Alumni Society to one alumni in each of the following categories:

  • A graduate from any CEHD degree program with at least 15 years of work experience in preK-20 educational institutions (examples: preschools, ECFE, K-12 schools, colleges, universities, etc.). Alumni working at such preK-20 educational institutions can represent a variety of career disciplines (examples: teachers, principals, administrators, counselors, psychologists, coaches, social workers, staff, faculty, activity directors, etc.).
  • A graduate from any CEHD degree program with at least 15 years of work experience outside of preK-20 educational institutions, representing any career discipline.

    • Contact the CEHD Alumni Society for further information: cehdas@umn.edu

    • 2015: Kenneth Dragseth, Amelia Franck Meyer
    • 2014: Kenneth Mauer, Barbara Schmidt
    • 2013: Marvin Davis, Peg Lonnquist
