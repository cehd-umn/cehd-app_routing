Alumni Society

When you join the University of Minnesota Alumni Association, you will receive a free membership in the College of Education and Human Development Alumni Society.

A portion of your dues to the University of Minnesota Alumni Association is shared with the CEHD Alumni Society, enabling us to provide important programs and services, including student scholarships.

The CEHD Alumni Society has over 8,000 members and is the second largest alumni society at the University.
