Message from the Alumni Society President

As president of the CEHD Alumni Society, I extend a special invitation to you to join the 75,000 members of the CEHD alumni Society along with the 582,000 members of the University of Minnesota Alumni Association. By joining the Alumni Association you will also automatically become a member of the CEHD Alumni Society.

Stay connected and informed. Participate in activities throughout the year. Maintain your association with one of the top-rated colleges of education and human development in the country.

Wherever your career path has led you, your “home” will always be the University of Minnesota.

Sincerely,

Chris Dixon, M.Ed. 2005, B.S. 2004
CEHD Alumni Society President
