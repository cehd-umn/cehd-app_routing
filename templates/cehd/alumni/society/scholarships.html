Alumni Society student scholarships

The College of Education and Human Development Alumni Society offers scholarships for undergraduate and graduate students in the college. The awards listed below are administered centrally as part of the college-wide scholarship process. Please visit CEHD Undergraduate Scholarships for information about deadlines and how to apply.

  • Promise of Tomorrow Scholarship for undergraduate transfer students in any CEHD program and for students in the initial licensure program.
  • Alumni Society Scholarship for Study Abroad for students who intend to study abroad, particularly underrepresented students and students who are among the first generation of their families to attend college.
  • Larry Wilson Endowment in Education for undergraduate or graduate students with a career objective related to non-school based education.
  • Alumni Society Family Social Science Future Scholar Fund for upper-division undergraduates who are pursuing degrees in the Department of Family Social Science.

The Alumni Society also provides the Advanced Study Scholarship for students enrolled in masters and doctoral programs in CEHD. This fund is being used for emergency/crisis grants; please contact your department's graduate student coordinator for more information.
