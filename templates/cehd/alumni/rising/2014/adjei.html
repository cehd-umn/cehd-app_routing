Millicent Adjei
2014 Rising Alumni

Associate Director of Diversity and International Programs
Ashesi University College

Accra, Ghana
CEHD degree: M.A., Comparative and International Development Education

Millicent Adjei is a diversity and international education leader at Ashesi University College, Ghana's only liberal arts college. She is currently part of the team executing a $13 million MasterCard Foundation Scholars Program at the college. Millicent works extensively with the admissions team to select some of the brightest young minds in Ghana and across Africa who are financially underprivileged but academically brilliant to study at Ashesi. She is also a board member for the local Girls' Exploration and Empowerment Club, an after school learning center for underprivileged girls.

What skills are important to succeed as a young professional today?

Compassion for others, continuous learning, emotional intelligence, creativity, an open mindset, integrity, and a commitment to responsibility.

What professors were most influential during your time in CEHD?

Definitely Frances Vavrus. She is simply fantastic and extremely supportive and will push you to produce nothing but excellence. The two years of mentorship I received from Professor Vavrus greatly shaped my attitude to pursue quality and excellence in my work.

When you have 30 minutes of free time, what do you do?

I love being brought to speed on what's up in the lives of my 9 and 6 year old daughters. I spend every chance I get with them just chatting.

If you could have coffee with anyone from history, who would it be?

I've always been fascinated and curious by Michelangelo's painting of the scenes from Genesis and The Last Judgment, so it will probably be with him.

What was the impact and benefit of your experience in CEHD?

CEHD's Comparative and International Development Education program gave me broad knowledge about the scope of international education and the various players involved. This knowledge is really handy in my current work at Ashesi. My internship and work with the ISSS further gave me the platform to learn and gain experience from a world-class university. Finally, CEHD gave me access to a strong network of colleagues and professors, many of whom I maintain a close relationship with for professional support in my daily work.
