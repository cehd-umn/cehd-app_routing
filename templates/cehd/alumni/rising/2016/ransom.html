Lakeesha Ransom2016 Rising Alumni

Lakeesha Ransom is a rising star in university administration. Lakeesha has a master’s and PhD in organizational leadership, and has reached a very senior academic leadership position early in her career. She is currently the vice provost and the dean of the Honors College at the University of Akron, after being in a similar position at the University of Toledo. Lakeesha served on the University of Minnesota Board of Regents for two terms and remains active on boards of community organizations.
