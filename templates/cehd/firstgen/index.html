#FirstGen Institute

Making an institutional commitment

An event for UMN faculty, staff, and graduate students.

Friday, November 2, 2018
8:30 AM - 4:30 PM
Coffman Memorial Union

Registration is now closed.

Through our collective commitment to first-gen students, the First Generation Institute will:

  • Strengthen participants' knowledge of what it means to be a first-generation college student.
  • Examine the strengths and challenges of different first-generation student populations, and cultivate the specific opportunities that will help their members to thrive.
  • Highlight programmatic, curricular, and co-curricular practices to narrow opportunity gaps throughout college.
  • Provide the opportunity for colleagues to collectively brainstorm the most effective ways of working with first-generation students to help them persist and thrive in the UMN system.
