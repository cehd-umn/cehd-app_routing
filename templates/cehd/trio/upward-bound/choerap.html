About Tenzin

Tenzin was born as a refugee in India and immigrated to the United States with his family. Tenzin earned his bachelor's of arts degree from St. Olaf College. During his undergraduate studies, he studied abroad in Budapest, Hungary and participated in both TRIO Student Support Services and the McNair Scholars program. Upon his graduation from St. Olaf, he worked in the insurance industry for three years. While he worked full time in the insurance industry, he also volunteered his time in the Tibetan community based in Twin Cities helping first generation Tibetan high school students navigate high school career.

In 2014, Tenzin joined the TRIO Student Support Services (SSS) program at St. Olaf College as the academic advisor and residential coordinator. In his role there, he provided academic and career support for students, co-lead Board of Leaders, and served on multiple committees relating to scholarships and STEM programs. In spring of 2019, he joined TRIO Upward Bound at the University of Minnesota as the College Access Advisor and Residential Coordinator. To further his understanding and deepening his knowledge of working with students, he will start his master’s degree in the Department of Organizational Leadership, Policy and Development in higher education at the University of Minnesota in the fall of 2019.

Tenzin loves to travel and explore different cultures. He has visited four out of seven continents and plans to do more traveling as time permits. He likes to stay active by going to the gym, biking, playing basketball and football, and hiking. There is not one food that Tenzin will not try at least once!
