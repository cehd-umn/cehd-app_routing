About Jonathan

Jonathan wandered into the Twin Cities three years ago, after conducting ecology research at Dartmouth College. He’s currently pursuing a Ph.D. in science education at the U of M. His dissertation focuses on how to improve gender equity in college biology courses. During the academic year, he teaches EDHD 1620, an integrated learning biology course that aims to help students develop their scientific literacy skills. In his spare time he enjoys reading about American politics and history and loves to bake. His favorite desserts are lemon-based.
