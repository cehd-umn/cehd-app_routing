About Jonathan

I teach two sections of EDHD-1620: Integrated Learning in Biology. I am currently a PhD student in Science Education, with a passion for teaching and all things biology. Originally from Guatemala, I have done evolutionary biology research at Dartmouth College and Indiana University. If you're interested in quirky biological events and enjoy talking about the intersection of science and society, you should take my course!
