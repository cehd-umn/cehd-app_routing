About Tabatha

Hi all! My name is Tabatha Cruz and I am a first-generation PhD student in the Higher Education program at the U. I moved around a lot growing up and call many places home including New York, Puerto Rico, Texas, and Iowa. Some topics I enjoy studying are pop culture, first-generation college students, and student activism.

I received my Associate's degree in Psychology from Palo Alto Community College, a Bachelor's degree in Family Services from the University of Northern Iowa, and a Master's degree in College Student Personnel Administration from Illinois State University.

I am excited to join the TRIO SSS team by serving as the Integrated Learning Psychology Instructor for EDHD 1620: Section 1.
