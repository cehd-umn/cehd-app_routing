About Melizza

Meliszza started her career at Minneapolis Community and Technical College and earned her Associates Degree in Human Services. During this time, she was a work study in an administrative assistant role in the TRIO program with the Educational Opportunity Center (EOC). She had the privilege to show students how to complete FAFSA applications and gave advice about college access. She believes college access is vitally important for youth. She worked in the Office of Financial Aid at St. Catherine University for ten years, assisting students through the financial aid process. Most recently, she worked at Genesys Works Twin CIties as an Administrative Coordinator, referred to as “Mom of the office”. In all the years of her career, the most important part of her job is working with students and witnessing them overcome obstacles they didn’t think they could. She believes if we invest in our children & young adults, the community will be empowered and successful. She started her career in higher education in a TRIO office and is honored to be a part of the TRIO program once again.

She is a mother of two and in her spare time she loves to garden and work on house projects. Her absolute favorite place to find peace is the shores of Lake Superior.
