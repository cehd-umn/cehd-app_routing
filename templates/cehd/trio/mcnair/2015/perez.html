Her research interests revolve around youth, race, institutional inequalities, and access to higher education. Ms. Perez plans on getting her Ph.D. in either higher or urban education.

My dream is to continue my educational journey and receive a Ph.D. in order to better serve my community. I dream that my education will help prepare me in creating a bridge program for underrepresented high school students in the Minneapolis and surrounding area.

Research Project

Rethinking Cultural Competence

Abstract: Within the last 20 years there have been various models and tools created that attempt to accurately assess a persons cultural and intercultural competence/awareness. There have been many attempts to try and accurately define this concept, but a consensus has yet to be reached (Johnson, 2006). With so many cultural competence assessments available it is difficult to figure out which cultural competence assessment is best. The overall purpose of this study was to compare four distinct cultural competence assessment tools, and their respective developmental plans. By developing a qualitative critical case study that reviewed current literature pertaining to cultural competence assessments, and their effectiveness over time, this research discovered many common elements between the assessments. Due to this, it is difficult to critique them against one another. Download poster. [PDF]

Faculty Profile

Jarrett Gupton is currently an assistant professor in the Higher Education program in the Department of Organizational Leadership, Policy, and Development at the University of Minnesota. Gupton attended the University of Southern California, which is where he received his Ph.D. in urban educational policy with a focus in higher education in 2009. His research specializes in access to higher education, equity in higher education, educational opportunity, and qualitative methods. Gupton is published in multiple research journals and has presented his work at conferences nationwide. This is Gupton’s first year of being a McNair faculty mentor.
