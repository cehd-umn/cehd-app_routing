CEHD statement following the death of Minneapolis resident George Floyd

As a CEHD community, we call out the historical and current racism in our society, the disparities and inequities we know exist, and the challenges to adequately address them and affect change. We are deeply affected by the realization that our society is one in which an unarmed, handcuffed black man can die after being choked by an officer on the streets of Minneapolis who is protected by other officers standing guard, who is unfazed by it being captured on video, and who doesn’t relent until the paramedics arrive. This is unacceptable.

President Gabel’s recent message regarding the tragic death of George Floyd identifies that as a community we are outraged, grief-stricken, and words alone cannot capture the anger and pain we are feeling. The president also addressed that the University is taking actions to limit collaboration with the Minneapolis Police Department and cease contracting them for additional services. We support these steps and we thank President Gabel for her leadership and action.

We acknowledge that more must be done. As a college, we have a deep commitment to equity and social justice. This commitment is not just what we say, but it is what we do in our teaching, research, and service to our communities. We are being called upon now more than ever to reaffirm and expand this commitment. We are being called upon now more than ever to strengthen ourselves and our communities. We are being called upon now more than ever to find new ways in which our work can impact change. As we convey messages of equity, diversity, and inclusion and as this is continuously reflected in our work, let us also identify new ways to engage in anti-racism actions, and policy and institution redesign.

We are a college of compassionate teachers, researchers, and leaders. We can do more. We can have greater impact, we can continue to build partnerships, and we can engage with each other and community leaders to implement change. We can and we must.

Our hearts will remain heavy and our thoughts with George Floyd, his family, and his friends.

Take care of yourselves and take care of each other.
