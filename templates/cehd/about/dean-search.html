National Search for Dean

The University of Minnesota is conducting a national search for a new dean of the College of Education and Human Development. A search committee has been named by the executive vice president and provost. The University has engaged Sean Farrell and Ericka Miller of the national search firm Isaacson, Miller to assist the search committee in the recruitment and screening of candidates. A position profile will be posted in early spring 2020.

Listening Sessions, Feb. 27–28

Opportunities to provide input on the position profile and search process:
Times and locations will be posted by Wed., Feb. 19.
