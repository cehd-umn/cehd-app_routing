National Search for Dean

National Search for Dean Postponed

In light of the evolving challenges of the COVID-19, the process of searching for a new Dean of Education and Human Development has been postponed.

The University of Minnesota in early 2020 launched a national search for a new dean of the College of Education and Human Development. A search committee was named by the executive vice president and provost, with Sean Farrell and Ericka Miller of the national search firm Isaacson, Miller engaged to assist in the recruitment and screening of candidates.
