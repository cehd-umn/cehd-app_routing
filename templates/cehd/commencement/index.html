Commencement

CEHD commencement is held once a year in the spring. No tickets are necessary, and seating is available on a first-come basis. 3M Arena at Mariucci has ample seating for any number of guests.

Undergraduate commencement

The 2018 CEHD undergraduate student commencement ceremony will be held Thursday, May 10, 2018, from 5:00-7:30 p.m. at 3M Arena at Mariucci.

Participating in commencement

  1. RSVP. The deadline to RSVP is Monday, April 23, 2018.
  2. Students can order their cap and gown at Gradfest ‘18, which is Wednesday, March 21, 2018 from 10:00 a.m. until 6:00 p.m. and Thursday, March 22, 2018 from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. in the Great Hall of Coffman Union. Students can also order their cap, gown, and tassel through the University of Minnesota Bookstores.
  3. Apply to be the undergraduate commencement student speaker. The deadline is Monday, April 2.
  4. If your participation in commencement would not be possible without financial assistance, consider applying for a commencement scholarship. Limited funds are available, and scholarships will be given based on financial need, the amount of scholarship funds available, and the reasons you provide for needing assistance. Complete the application by Friday, April 6.

More information

For more information about graduation requirements, contact your undergraduate adviser.

Graduate commencement

The 2018 CEHD graduate student commencement ceremony will be held Thursday, May 10, 2018, from 12:30-3:00 p.m. at 3M Arena at Mariucci.

Participating in commencement

  1. Request participation in commencement. The deadline to request approval to participate in the commencement ceremony is Monday, April 23, 2018.
  2. Students can order their cap and gown at Gradfest ‘18, which is Wednesday, March 21, 2018 from 10:00 a.m. until 6:00 p.m. and Thursday, March 22, 2018 from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. in the Great Hall of Coffman Union. Students can also order their master's and doctoral apparel through the University of Minnesota Bookstores.
  3. If your participation in commencement would not be possible without financial assistance, consider applying for a commencement scholarship. Limited funds are available, and scholarships will be given based on financial need, the amount of scholarship funds available, and the reasons you provide for needing assistance. Complete the application by Friday, April 6.
