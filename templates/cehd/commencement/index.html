Commencement

CEHD commencement is held once a year in the spring. The 2019 graduate and undergraduate commencement ceremonies will be held Wednesday, May 8, 2019.

  • Graduate Ceremony: 11:00 a.m.
  • Undergraduate Ceremony: 4:30 p.m.

No tickets are necessary, and seating is available on a first-come basis. 3M Arena at Mariucci has ample seating for any number of guests.
