Undergraduate commencement

The 2018 CEHD undergraduate student commencement ceremony will be held Thursday, May 10, 2018, from 5:00-7:30 p.m. at 3M Arena at Mariucci.

Participating in commencement

  1. The deadline to RSVP has passed. Please contact your undergraduate adviser if you have questions about participating in the ceremony.
  2. Students may order their cap, gown, and tassel through the University of Minnesota Bookstores.

Student check-in and ceremony details

Please check-in at 4:15 p.m. to Mariucci Arena’s loading dock area located at 1912 5th Street SE. You will pick up your reader’s card and line-up by department. Commencement coordinators will be on hand to provide direction on where to line up for the procession. Procession into the arena starts at 5:00 p.m.

Preparation

  • Bring (or wear) gown, cap, and tassel
  • Leave all coats, bags, and valuables with your guests, as there will not be a secure place for you to leave them. If you do have any personal items, please make sure they are under your robe. Because Mariucci Arena is big, we suggest that you decide on a specific location to meet your family and friends after the ceremony.
  • You may want to eat before you come to the ceremony. There is a lot of waiting in line and then sitting during the ceremony, and we do not have food or water available for all. Please note that there are only limited restrooms for use before the ceremony.
  • Please also share with your guests that balloons are not allowed in Mariucci Arena.
  • Note that CEHD has professional photographers taking photos during the ceremony for use in CEHD publications and websites. Grad Images will take an individual photo of you on the stage, and you will receive a free proof of this photo in the mail within six days following the ceremony. There is no obligation to purchase.
  • The ceremony will be available live online for any guests who are unable to attend in person.

Ceremony

  • Tassel should start on the right side of cap/face and then moved to left after proceeding across the stage.
  • Each row will be signaled by an usher to stand and proceed towards the stage.
  • Give your reader’s card to the reader or commencement coordinator; you may be asked to whisper your name to the reader. Name is read. Proceed across the stage.
  • Greet the Dean and Regents. We are a diverse community and we respect and welcome students who do not wish to shake hands due to personal, cultural, or religious beliefs. We ask that students extend a hand first to express their wish to shake hands.
  • Receive diploma cover; official diplomas are mailed to students from the Office of the Registrar and will not be in the diploma cover during the ceremony.
  • An individual commencement picture will be taken while exiting the stage.
  • Return to seat.

More information

For more information about graduation requirements, contact your undergraduate adviser.

Graduate commencement

The 2018 CEHD graduate student commencement ceremony will be held Thursday, May 10, 2018, from 12:30-3:00 p.m. at 3M Arena at Mariucci.

Participating in commencement

  1. The deadline to RSVP has passed. Please contact your department's graduate program coordinator if you have questions about participating in commencement.
  2. Students may order their master's and doctoral apparel through the University of Minnesota Bookstores.

Student check-in and ceremony details

Please check-in at 11:45 a.m. to Mariucci Arena’s loading dock area located at 1912 5th Street SE. Procession into the arena starts at 12:30 p.m. At check-in you will pick up your reader’s card, which will be color coded by degree. Within each department, line-up Masters of Education (1st), Specialist Certificate (2nd), Masters of Arts/Masters of Science/Masters of Social Work (3rd), Doctor of Education/EdD (4th), Doctor of Philosophy/PhD (5th). Commencement staff will also assist you with line-up.

Preparation

  • Bring (or wear) gown, cap, tassel and U of M hood to ceremony. Masters students can wear their hoods. Doctoral students will be hooded on stage. Hoods have a loop that attaches to a shirt button, so you may want to wear a shirt with buttons under your robe.
  • Leave all coats, bags, and valuables with your guests, as there will not be a secure place for you to leave them. If you do have any personal items, please make sure they are under your robe. Because Mariucci Arena is big, we suggest that you decide on a specific location to meet your family and friends after the ceremony.
  • You may want to eat before you come to the ceremony. There is a lot of waiting in line and then sitting during the ceremony, and we do not have food or water available for all. Please note that there are only limited restrooms for use before the ceremony.
  • Please also share with your guests that balloons are not allowed in Mariucci Arena.
  • Note that CEHD has professional photographers taking photos during the ceremony for use in CEHD publications and websites. Grad Images will take an individual photo of you on the stage, and you will receive a free proof of this photo in the mail within six days following the ceremony. There is no obligation to purchase.
  • The ceremony will be available live online for any guests who are unable to attend in person.

Ceremony

  • Masters students: Tassel will start on the right side of cap/face and moved to left after going across the stage. Doctoral students: Tassel starts on left and hood will be pinned on stage. Doctoral students should carry their hoods draped over their left arms in the procession.
  • Each row will be signaled by an usher to stand and proceed towards the stage.
  • Give your reader’s card to the reader or commencement coordinator; you may be asked to whisper your name to the reader. Name is read. Proceed across the stage.
  • Greet the Dean and Regents. We are a diverse community and we respect and welcome students who do not wish to shake hands due to personal, cultural, or religious beliefs. We ask that students extend a hand first to express their wish to shake hands.
  • Doctoral students will have their hood pinned. Receive diploma cover; official diplomas are mailed to students from the Office of the Registrar and will not be in the diploma cover at the ceremony.
  • An individual commencement picture will be taken while exiting the stage.
  • Return to seat.
