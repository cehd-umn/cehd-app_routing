Commencement

CEHD commencement is held once a year in the spring. No tickets are necessary, and seating is available on a first-come basis. 3M Arena at Mariucci has ample seating for any number of guests.

Guest information

Find more information for guests regarding directions and parking.

2019 CEHD commencement

Wednesday, May 8, 2019

Commencement is held at 3M Arena at Mariucci, 1901 4th Street Southeast, Minneapolis, MN 55455

  • Graduate Ceremony: 11:00 a.m.-1:30 p.m.
  • Undergraduate Ceremony: 4:30-7:00 p.m.

Graduate commencement

The 2019 CEHD graduate commencement ceremony will be held Wednesday, May 8, 2019, 11:00 a.m.-1:30 p.m. at 3M Arena at Mariucci.

Participating in commencement

  1. Submit your request to attend commencement. Graduate students need approval. You will receive a confirmation email within five business days after your submission. The deadline to request approval to participate in the commencement ceremony is Monday, April 22, 2019.
  2. Order your cap and gown through the University of Minnesota Bookstores.
