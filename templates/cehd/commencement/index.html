Commencement

On May 16, the University of Minnesota will honor all 2020 undergraduate and graduate students during a virtual commencement. Join us at 11 a.m. CDT for an online celebration. The theme for the celebration is “Hail to Thee,” the University’s alma mater and a reference from Minnesota’s state song chosen to reflect the University’s admiration for your incredible resilience in these stressful times. Graduates will receive an email from MarchingOrder to provide a photo that will be displayed with their graduation information on the commencement site. For those planning to graduate this spring or fall, if you don't receive an email by April 24, please let us know. Graduating students are encouraged to use #HailtoThee2020 in their commencement social posts and participate in a cap decorating contest, #CapsOff2020. Branded Zoom backgrounds, cap decorating instructions, other assets and commencement information are available at z.umn.edu/commencement2020.

CEHD will host a virtual ceremony in conjunction with the U of M systemwide ceremony. Further details will be posted soon.
