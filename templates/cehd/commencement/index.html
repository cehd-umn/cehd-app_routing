Commencement

CEHD commencement is held once a year in the spring. No tickets are necessary, and seating is available on a first-come basis. 3M Arena at Mariucci has ample seating for any number of guests.

Speakers

Graduate ceremony: William J. Doherty, Professor, Department of Family Social Science, Director of the Citizen Professional Center, and Director of the Minnesota Couples on the Brink Project

Undergraduate ceremony: Michael Goh, Interim Vice President, Office for Equity and Diversity and Professor, Department of Organizational Leadership​, Policy, and Development

Undergraduate commencement

The 2018 CEHD undergraduate student commencement ceremony will be held Thursday, May 10, 2018, from 5:00-7:30 p.m. at 3M Arena at Mariucci.

Participating in commencement

  1. RSVP. The deadline to RSVP is Monday, April 23, 2018.
  2. Students may order their cap, gown, and tassel through the University of Minnesota Bookstores.

Graduate commencement
