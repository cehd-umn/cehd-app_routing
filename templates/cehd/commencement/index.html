Commencement

CEHD commencement is held once a year in the spring. No tickets are necessary, and seating is available on a first-come basis. 3M Arena at Mariucci has ample seating for any number of guests.

Graduate commencement

The 2019 CEHD graduate commencement ceremony will be held Wednesday, May 8, 2019, 11:00 a.m.-1:30 p.m. at 3M Arena at Mariucci.

Participating in commencement

  1. The deadline to RSVP has passed. Please contact your department's graduate program coordinator.
  2. Order your cap and gown through the University of Minnesota Bookstores.

Ceremony instructions

Please check-in at 10:15 a.m. to Mariucci Arena’s loading dock area located at 1912 5th Street SE. Procession into the arena starts at 11:00 a.m.

Preparation

  • Bring (or wear) gown, cap, tassel and U of M hood to ceremony. Masters students can wear their hoods. Doctoral students will be hooded on stage.
  • Once hoods are on they have a loop that attaches to a shirt button, so you may want to wear a shirt with buttons under your robe.
  • Leave all coats, bags and valuables with your guests as there will not be a secure place for you to leave them. If you do have any personal items, please make sure they are under your robe.
  • Because Mariucci Arena is big, we suggest that you decide on a specific location to meet your family and friends after the ceremony.
  • You may want to eat before you come to the ceremony. There is a lot of waiting in line and then sitting during the ceremony, and we do not have food or water available for all. Please note that there are only limited restrooms for use before the ceremony.
  • Please review guest information, and share with your guests balloons are not allowed in 3M at Mariucci Arena.
  • Note that CEHD has professional photographers taking photos during the ceremony for use in CEHD publications and websites.
  • Grad Images will take an individual photo of you on the stage, and you will receive a free proof of this photo within six days following the ceremony. There is no obligation to purchase.
  • The ceremony will be available live online.

Check-in and Line-up

  • Check-in happens in the hallway that starts just inside the Loading Dock. Look for the table with the alphabet bunch for your last name.
  • Pick up your reader’s card.
  • Proceed through the hall to the main floor.
  • Line-up by department and by degree type within the department (starting with Master’s degrees, ending with PhDs). Commencement coordinators will be on hand to provide direction on where to line up for the procession. Refer to this map for where your department will be seated in preparation for the procession. It is important you get into that section and stay in that seat until the procession begins. It is important you get into that section and stay in that seat until the procession begins.

Procession

  • The procession into the arena will start at 11:00 a.m., and the ceremony will begin immediately after. The entire ceremony will be approximately two and a half hours.

Ceremony

  • Masters students: Tassel will start on the right side of cap/face and moved to left after going across the stage.
  • Doctoral students: Tassel starts on left and hood will be pinned on stage. Doctoral students should carry their hoods draped over their left arms in the procession.
  • Each row will be signaled by an usher to stand and proceed towards the stage.
  • Give your reader’s card to the reader or commencement coordinator; you may be asked to whisper your name to the reader.
  • Name is read.
  • Proceed across the stage.
  • Greet the Dean, Regent, and Faculty. We are a diverse community and we respect and welcome students who do not wish to shake hands due to personal, cultural, or religious beliefs. We ask that students extend a hand first to express their wish to shake hands.
  • Doctoral students will have their hood pinned. Receive diploma cover; official diplomas are mailed to students from the Office of the Registrar and will not be in the diploma cover at the ceremony.
  • Return to seat.
