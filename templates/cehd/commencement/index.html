Commencement

CEHD commencement is held once a year in the spring. No tickets are necessary, and seating is available on a first-come basis. 3M Arena at Mariucci has ample seating for any number of guests. Find more information for guests.

2018 CEHD commencement

Thursday, May 10, 2018

Graduate ceremony, 12:30 - 3:00 p.m.
Undergraduate ceremony, 5:00 - 7:30 p.m.

3M Arena at Mariucci
1901 4th Street SE
Minneapolis, MN 55455

Undergraduate commencement

The 2018 CEHD undergraduate student commencement ceremony will be held Thursday, May 10, 2018, from 5:00-7:30 p.m. at 3M Arena at Mariucci.

Participating in commencement

  1. Undergraduate students will receive an email invitation to participate in commencement in mid-February. This email will have the link for an online RSVP, which will also be posted on this website. The deadline to RSVP is Monday, April 23, 2018.
  2. Students can order their cap and gown at Gradfest ‘18, which is Wednesday, March 21, 2018 from 10:00 a.m. until 6:00 p.m. and Thursday, March 22, 2018 from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. in the Great Hall of Coffman Union. Students can also order their cap, gown, and tassel through the University of Minnesota Bookstores.

More information

For more information about graduation requirements, contact your undergraduate adviser.

Graduate commencement

The 2018 CEHD graduate student commencement ceremony will be held Thursday, May 10, 2018, from 12:30-3:00 p.m. at 3M Arena at Mariucci.

Participating in commencement

  1. Graduate students need approval to attend commencement. Students will receive an email invitation to request participation in commencement in mid-February. They will receive a confirmation email within five business days after their submission. The deadline to request approval to participate in the commencement ceremony is Monday, April 23, 2018.
  2. Students can order their cap and gown at Gradfest ‘18, which is Wednesday, March 21, 2018 from 10:00 a.m. until 6:00 p.m. and Thursday, March 22, 2018 from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. in the Great Hall of Coffman Union. Students can also order their master's and doctoral apparel through the University of Minnesota Bookstores.
