Disability accommodations

Guests may be dropped off at the entrance on the northeast corner of the Mariucci Arena (access to this area is located between 5th and Oak Streets). There are a limited number of parking spots for ADA guests in Lot 37.

Wheelchair seating is reserved in two areas in Mariucci Arena, and is first-come, first-served. Refer to the options below and map [PDF] of Mariucci. 

  • Option 1: Wheelchair seating is available at the top of sections 2, 3, and 24, and these sections have additional seating for other friends and family. These sections are the closest to the main doors (east side of the building facing Oak Street).

    Directions:     When first entering the building, go directly to the elevator located on the left side of the main lobby; take it to the second floor (concourse level), and the sections are straight ahead.
  • Option 2: The Gopher Bench area is also available to wheelchairs. This area is limited to the guest with a wheelchair and one friend/family member. It is almost level with the ice, so the visibility may be slightly limited. Access to the commencement area is not permitted.

    Entering from Oak Street into Main Lobby: Take the elevator from the main lobby to the 2nd floor (concourse level); turn left and go to section 11 elevator; take this elevator to Ice Level (L); go straight down the hall and take the first right; follow signs to room 4 hockey bench area.

    Entering from 4th Street: Take this elevator to Ice Level (L); go straight down the hall and take the first right; follow signs to room 4 hockey bench area

For ASL interpreters, please contact the Disability Resource Center, drc@umn.edu, at least four weeks prior to commencement.

Commencement speakers

Keynote speaker

Abdul Wright is an eighth grade English language arts teacher at Best Academy in north Minneapolis. He was the 2016 Minnesota Teacher of the Year and the first African American man and the youngest person to receive the award. He is also the first teacher from a charter school to receive the award. Mr. Wright strives to be an educational leader in the classroom and in the community. He is a voice for underrepresented populations. He received the Minneapolis PeaceMaker's Award from the city of Minneapolis in 2015 and is a six-time recipient of the Cristo Rey Jesuit High School “You've Made a Difference” award. Mr. Wright earned a bachelor’s degree in communication arts and literature from Concordia University-St. Paul and a master’s degree in education from Hamline University. He also has completed an African American leadership program.

Undergraduate student speaker

Candace Bunyan is graduating with a bachelor of science degree in elementary education foundations. She currently holds five positions, two on campus and three off campus in the Minneapolis community. Some of her roles are being a peer coach for the Center of Academic Planning and Exploration, lab coordinator at the Multicultural Center for Academic Excellence, History Day mentor, Beacons after-school coordinator, and graduation coach at her alma mater, Edison Senior High School. She received the 2017 Social Justice Pre-Service Teacher Award for her actions in removing educational barriers and recognizing the inequities within our educational system. With her dedication and commitment, she has been addressing social justice issues, educational equity, and role-modeling leadership on and off campus. This fall, she will enter the master’s program in elementary education at CEHD on a full scholarship that she earned through her notable efforts in education.
