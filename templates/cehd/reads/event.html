CEHD ReadsFirst Generation Panel Discussion

Thursday, October 10, 2019 | 7:30 - 9:00 p.m.
Northrop Memorial Auditorium

{{ include('cehd/reads/aa-nav.html') }}

Join the CEHD community as we welcome a panel of first-generation professionals to share their multifaceted experiences as the first in their family to attend college. First-generation students and professionals have not only overcome barriers including cultural, financial, and academic, but they also bring many strengths to the classroom and beyond. Panelists will also share their thoughts on the 2019-20 CEHD Reads book, Make Your Home Among Strangers, by Jennine Capó Crucet, an autobiographical novel about a first-generation student going to college. The panel will be moderated by Associate Professor Rashné Jehangir. Free and open to the public.
