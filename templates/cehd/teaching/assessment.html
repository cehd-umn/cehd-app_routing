Assessment and Accreditation

CAEP Annual Reporting and Monitoring

As part of our accreditation from CAEP, we publish information and statistics about students in our initial licensure programs (ILP).

Measures of Program Impacts

97.4% of 2014/15 candidates who completed the program agree or tend to agree that they would would recommend the ILP to other interested candidates.
(from 2013 Candidate Exit Survey, response rate 78.8%)

Employers agreed or tended to agree that first year teachers from our ILP programs:

  • Creates a learning environment in which differences such as race, culture, gender, sexual orientation, and language are respected (92.2%)
  • Regularly adjusts instructional plans to meet students' needs (89.1%)
  • Designs instructions to support students who are English language learners (89.6%)
  • Has developed a classroom environment that promotes student engagement (85.3%)
  • Effectively teaches students from culturally and ethnically diverse backgrounds and communities (92.6%)
  • Actively engages parents/guardians in support of their children's learning (88.9%)

(from Supervisor-Employer Survey 2013-2014, response rate 67%)

Initial Licensure Program

  • 2014/2015: 351 new enrollments, 261 candidates completed
  • 2013/2014: 303 new enrollments, 294 candidates completed
  • 2012/2013: 246 new enrollments, 284 candidates completed

Candidates may take more than a year to complete the program. For more information on initial licensure program enrollments and completion, see Title II Reports.
