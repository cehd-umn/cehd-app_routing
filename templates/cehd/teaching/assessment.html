Assessment and accreditation

Annual reporting and monitoring

As part of our continuous improvement effort, we publish information and statistics about students in our initial licensure programs (ILP).

Measures of program impacts

88.3% of 2015/16 candidates who completed the program agree or tend to agree that they would recommend the ILP to other interested candidates.
(from 2017 Transition to Teaching Survey of 2015/16 completers, response rate 87.1%)

Employers agreed or tended to agree that first year teachers from our ILP programs:

  • Effectively teaches students from culturally and ethnically diverse backgrounds and communities.
  • Uses effective communication skills and strategies to convey ideas and information to students.
  • Creates a learning environment in which differences such as race, culture, gender, sexual orientation, and language are respected.
  • Collaborates with teaching colleagues to improve student performance.
  • Acts as an advocate for all students.
  • Effectively organizes the physical environment of the classroom for instruction.

(Office of Teacher Education Initial Analysis of 2015/16 Common Metrics Supervisor (Employer) Survey. 71.8% response rate. These survey questions had the most favorable ratings by employers).

Exam pass rate of the Minnesota Teacher Licensure Exam (MTLE)

The Minnesota Teacher Licensure Examinations, or MTLEs, are tests taken by candidates as part of applying for a Minnesota teaching license. All candidates for initial licenses take tests in basic skills, content-area knowledge, and pedagogy.
