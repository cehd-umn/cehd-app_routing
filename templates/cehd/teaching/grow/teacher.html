MNGOT: M.Ed. and Initial Elementary Teaching License

Minnesota school districts have a goal to increase the racial and linguistic diversity of our teachers. In 2015, the College of Education and Human Development (CEHD) created a Multiple Pathways to Teaching initiative. Our objective is to diversify the teaching profession by creating new pathways to becoming a fully licensed teacher. We are expanding our Minneapolis Residency Program to include more districts and spreading out the time it takes to complete (from one year to two years). The 36-credit program is called the University of Minnesota Grow Your Own Teachers Program (MNGOT).

Is this program right for you?

The University of Minnesota Grow Your Own Teachers Program is designed for individuals who have an undergraduate degree and are currently employed in a partner district, including:

  • Educational assistants or paraprofessionals
  • Substitute/reserve teachers who hold only a substitute teaching license
  • Other teachers who are teaching on a district waiver, but who do not have another US teaching licenser

Advantages of this program

  • Stay in residence at current employer
  • A teacher community (cohort) learning together for 2 years
  • Face-to-face summer courses and hybrid (online and face-to-face) courses during the academic year
  • Practicing teachers can complete the program while continuing to work
  • University/district agreement to facilitate a leave of absence for student teaching; possibility of paid student teaching if funding allows.
  • Innovative, equity-driven curriculum
  • University coursework concurrent with field-based experiences
  • University supervision in all school placements throughout the program

Become a partner district

Work with us to recruit new teachers from within your own district, increase the number of teachers of color and bilingual teachers in Minnesota, and contribute to the future of teacher preparation.

Partnership information

Get your license

If you have your bachelor's degree and work in one of our partner districts, you can complete the program in two years while continuing to work.

Program details

The Multiple Pathways to Teaching Office develops programs with local partners to address acute needs and specific goals. These programs include the the Minneapolis Residency Program, giving current Minneapolis Public Schools staff opportunities to pursue elementary teaching licensure.
