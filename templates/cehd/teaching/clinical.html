Clinical placements and partnerships

The Clinical Partnerships team is a subset of the Office of Teacher Education. The team’s goal is to deepen university-school partnerships to improve preK-12 student achievement. Major initiatives include:

  • enhancing student teaching/clinical experiences
  • improving mentoring of teacher candidates
  • researching topics related to partnership initiatives
  • expanding partnership networks.

Clinical supervisors

Clinical supervisors offer professional support and encouragement for teacher candidates during their pre-student teaching and student teaching experiences. You can find information on specific clinical supervisors in the Handbook.

Partnership liaisons

Partnership liaisons are site instructional leaders. They work to develop and support university partnerships that use student teaching/clinical experiences. Their responsibilities include:

  • supporting the selection and matching of teacher candidates with cooperating teachers (practicum and student teaching)
  • supporting teacher candidates and cooperating teachers as mentors
  • facilitating regular, on-site meetings and trainings
  • serving as the main point of communication with university placement coordinators and university partners
  • serving as point person for all university supervisors who support teacher candidates at site
  • understanding co-teaching strategies to support the clinical experience
  • assessing teacher candidates for early hire in school districts.
