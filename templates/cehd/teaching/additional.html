Additional or specialty teaching licenses

Our additional licenses are added to a current five-year, full-time professional Minnesota teaching license. If you want to add a new field or scope to your teaching, an additional license may be the path for you. Common additional licensure paths include, but are not limited to:

  • Elementary licensed teachers adding a middle school specialty area license.
  • Secondary licensed teachers adding a new secondary specialty area, or a middle school license in their current (or new) specialty area.
  • Additional licenses are also available in special education, English as a Second Language (ESL), world languages, and more.

Complete program requirements

Application information is listed by program. The number of credits required to complete a program varies based on past coursework. We will review your coursework after you apply.

Deadlines

  • October 1 to begin classes in spring semester (January)
  • February 1 to begin classes in summer semester (May/June)
  • March 1 for special education applicants only. This program admits students once a year to begin classes in summer of fall semesters.
  • June 1 to begin classes in fall semester (September)

Program areas

  • Adult Basic Education
  • Agricultural education (grades 5-12)
  • Arts in education (Dance, Theatre Arts, Visual Arts grades K-12)
  • Communication arts and literature education (grades 5-12)
  • Early childhood education (Birth - 3rd grade)
  • English as a second language [ESL] (grades K-12)
  • Mathematics education (grades 5-12)
  • Middle level (grades 5-8): communication arts and literature, Mathematics
  • Parent and family education
  • Reading (grades K-12)
  • The PELSB application for an Addition to a Minnesota Education License
  • Science education
    • chemistry (grades 9-12)
    • earth and space science (grades 9-12)
    • life science (grades 9-12)
    • physics (grades 9-12)
  • Special education
  • World languages and cultures (grades K-12)

Under federal financial aid rules, students enrolled in additional teaching licensure-only coursework have different aid options than students in a degree-seeking program at the University. More on additional licensure and financial aid [PDF].

Apply for your license

License requirements are set by the Professional Educator Licensing and Standards Board (PELSB) and are subject to change. If you are applying for an additional license in a new content area, you may be required to pass an MTLE content area exam.

Once you have completed all course requirements, additional state testing requirements (if needed), and all of your final grades are posted to your transcript, you can proceed with your application for an additional license. Your application packet must include:

  • Tier 4 Application (if you hold a MN 5 year standard or a Tier 4 License) or Tier 3 Application (if you hold a MN Tier 3 license)
    • Verification of Completion of a State-Approved Licensure Program Section: Fill out the Student Teaching/Practicum/Internship information
  • A copy of your current Minnesota teaching license.
  • A check made payable to the “Commissioner of Education”. The fee is listed on your application form.
  • Official transcripts, in sealed envelopes, from any other colleges or universities where you took coursework towards your additional license. A University of Minnesota transcript is not required.
  • Official MTLE test score results sheet, if you did not have them reported to the University of Minnesota. We will return your official score report to you.
  • Signed Transcript Release Form [PDF]
  • Completed CEHD Exit Survey sent to your UMN email.

Mail your complete application packet to:

Office of Teacher Education - Attn: Pam Matti
110 Wulling Hall 
86 Pleasant St. SE 
Minneapolis, MN 55455

We will review, sign, and send your application packet to the Professional Educator Licensing and Standards (PELSB). This usually takes about two weeks, but can take longer during the busy summer season. PELSB typically takes 8-12 weeks to process a license; you can track your license progress online through PELSB's website.

If you have further questions, please contact the Office of Teacher Education at ote@umn.edu.
