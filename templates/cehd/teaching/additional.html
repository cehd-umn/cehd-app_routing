Additional or specialty teaching licenses

Our additional licenses are added to a current five-year, full-time professional Minnesota teaching license. If you want to add a new field or scope to your teaching, an additional license may be the path for you. Common additional licensure paths include, but are not limited to:

  • Elementary licensed teachers adding a middle school specialty area license.
  • Secondary licensed teachers adding a new secondary specialty area, or a middle school license in their current (or new) specialty area.
  • Additional licenses are also available in special education, English as a Second Language (ESL), 14 world languages, and more.

How to apply

Applications are handled through your program area. The number of credits required to complete a program varies based on past coursework, which will be determined upon receipt of application.

Deadlines

  • October 1 to begin classes in spring semester (January)
  • February 1 to begin classes in summer semester (May/June)
  • March 1 for special education applicants only. This program admits students once a year to begin classes in summer of fall semesters.
  • June 1 to begin classes in fall semester (September)

Under federal financial aid rules, students enrolled in additional teaching licensure-only coursework have different aid options than students in a degree-seeking program at the University. More on additional licensure and financial aid [PDF].

Completing your license

License requirements are set by the Minnesota Board of Teaching and are subject to change. If you are applying for an additional license in a new content area, you may be required to pass an MTLE content area exam.

  • Arts in education (Dance, Theatre Arts, Visual Arts grades K-12)
  • Communication arts and literature education (grades 5-12)

  • Mathematics education (grades 5-12)
  • Middle level (grades 5-8): communication arts and literature, mathematics
  • Reading (grades K-12)
