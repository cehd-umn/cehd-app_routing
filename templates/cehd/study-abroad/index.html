Study and research abroad

Transform yourself and your future.

Time abroad is more than just travel. Experiencing a new culture first-hand will expand and deepen how you think about the world, and yourself.

In addition to fostering in-demand "soft skills" like empathy and cross-cultural communication, study abroad has been shown to contribute to better grades and on-time graduation.

Upcoming destinations

Teaching practicum in France

Study in this vibrant city with a centuries-old history of intellectual advances and social tolerance. Examine French education practices and complete your teaching practicum through teaching English in a French school.

Global change, community, and families in Thailand

Explore globalization, culture, families, and the natural world in Thailand.

English language acquisition methods in Brazil

Witness the challenge of building a modern education system in the developing country of Brazil. Through school visits, speakers and working in a classroom, you will examine how the country balances spending on these projects and other development needs while providing education services to its citizens.
