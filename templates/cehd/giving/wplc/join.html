Join us

WPLC, a collaborative community of leaders and philanthropists, works to enhance the lives of others. Join us in maximizing scholarship opportunities and celebrating the power of collective giving. Together, we can make a difference and foster a permanent legacy.

As a Circle member, you'll be able to:

  • Collaborate with a community of donors in making a difference.
  • Participate in educational programs.
  • Create a living and permanent legacy.
  • Celebrate the impact of your philanthropy with others.

    • Gifts are tax-deductible and can be made with check, credit card, electronic fund transfer (EFT), University of Minnesota payroll deduction, or through the transfer of appreciated securities. Contributions of any amount are welcome.

Membership

All who give, at any amount, to the WPLC Fund are considered friends of the Circle.
