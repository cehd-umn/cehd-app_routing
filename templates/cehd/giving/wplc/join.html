Join us

Women join WPLC at one of several tiered levels starting at $600 per year. Members meet several times each year to build relationships and to learn more about personal finance and philanthropy. Past WPLC workshops and seminars have touched on strategies for aging with vitality, differences in how men and women make financial decisions, and personal stories from women leaders on the “frontlines” of education and human development.

As a Circle member, you'll be able to:

  • Collaborate with a community of women in making a difference for other women.
  • Participate in educational programs.
  • Create a living and permanent legacy.
  • Celebrate the impact of your philanthropy with others.

    • Gifts are tax-deductible and can be made with check, credit card, electronic fund transfer (EFT), University of Minnesota payroll deduction, or through the transfer of appreciated securities. Contributions of any amount are welcome.
