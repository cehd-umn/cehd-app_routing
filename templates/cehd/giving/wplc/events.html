Events

WPLC hosts two events annually, a fall and summer awards celebration. Our events encourage social interaction and help to inspire philanthropic giving. We hope to see you at our next celebration. Don’t be shy, swing by!

Women's Philanthropic Leadership Circle
Fall Event

Inspire Philanthropy, Support Achievement, Foster Leadership

Tuesday, September 17, 2019, 5:30 - 7:30 PM
Coffman Union-Mississippi Room
Hors d'oeuvres, Beverages and Program

Featuring Jennifer Eggers, Director of Annual Giving at the University of Minnesota Foundation

How do you decide where to direct your charitable dollars? Do you expect giving online to be as easy as buying something on Amazon? What and how do you want to hear from the organizations you support? Have your giving habits changed over time? Join WPLC for networking and a discussion on how philanthropy is changing. Jennifer Eggers has worked at the UM Foundation since 2000, and is an expert in multi-channel solicitation strategies. She will talk about national giving trends, and how they are reflected within the Circle.

RSVP by September 12.
