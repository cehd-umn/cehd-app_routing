A note from Geoff: "Stanley Scramble" golf event & reception

As part of Stan's memorial celebration, we have included an event that we know Stan would have loved--a golf scramble. In a scramble, everyone on a team (4) hits, the team selects the best shot, and then everyone hits again, and the process repeats itself until the ball is in the hole, for 9 holes. So if you are even an occasional golfer, come have fun, for all we need from you is an occasional good shot and commitment to be a positive reinforcer for your teammates. The more golfers, the more fun! (Applied Behavior Analysis CEU credits are available.) We will pair teams up to balance them (as much as one can do with Doug golfing).

Following Stan, we expect everyone to dispute their handicap (yes, it's a number. No, it's not your AARP card or medical x-rays.) with the organizers throughout the event. As organizers, we will follow all of Stan's principles in organizing the event--so you'll want to come figure out what those are. We'll include string to improve scores and give mulligans to everyone who contributes to his memorial fund (in deference to Stan's ways, that means that everyone gets 1 mulligan for past or future expected contributions). Contingencies will be controlled by one's teammates, who are expected to positively reinforce every short, no matter how ugly.

How can you not participate? Ok, if you have never hit a golf ball in your life, then you get a pass.

Once the event is over (non-golfers, you are invited to come watch the "exciting" finish), we will gather at Geoff's house, along with other members of the Ed Psych department, for refreshments and food.

Details: Held Thursday May 25 at Highland 9-Hole Golf Course in Saint Paul. Green Fees $18 walking, $29 riding. Tee times begin at 1 p.m.
