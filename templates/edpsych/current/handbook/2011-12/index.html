2011-12 Graduate Student Handbook

Introduction and contacts

This handbook brings together important information that graduate students in the Department of Educational Psychology will find useful as they work towards their respective degree and certification goals. This handbook supplements the information provided in the Graduate School Catalog and specific track information. Your adviser will also provide you with information on requirements specific to your interests.

You are responsible for determining that you fulfill all degree requirements. Any changes in educational psychology core requirements will be distributed through the Educational Psychology Student Listserv.

If you have any questions concerning the educational psychology graduate program, your adviser is your first resource. However, you should feel free to get in touch with any of the resource people listed below. If you have questions about your particular program area (i.e., CSPP, QME, SchP, SpEd, or PsyF tracks), get in touch with the coordinator or a staff member in your area.
