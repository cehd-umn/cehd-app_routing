Quantitative methods in education
student travel funding

The quantitative methods in education program offers additional funding above and beyond the department award amounts. Eligible graduate students receive funding for student travel, in addition to department level funding, during the academic year.

Award amounts

Up to $500 of travel funds each academic year for national/international conferences ($300 for regional conferences), defined as the period September 1 to August 30.

Conditions

  • You must complete the QME Student Travel Funding Request Form [PDF], obtain your advisor's signature, and submit the form to Sharon Sawyer before you attend the conference.
  • Priority will be given to students who have authored/co-authored a conference presenrtation.
  • Funds can be used to pay for airfare or registration before or after the conference. Ground transportation, hotel, or food (per diem) costs can only be reimbused after the conference. See Sharon Sawyer for details.

How to get reimbursed

Upon return, present the following information to Sharon Sawyer within 30 days.

  • Proof of attendance (e.g., conference badge)
  • If you are an author or co-author, a copy of the conference program showing your presentation.
  • Original receipts for which you are requesting reimbursement. (Do not include meal receipts.)

You must submit your reimbursement request through Chrome River within 30 days of the conference. Contact Sharon Sawyer (sawye100@umn.edu) for account and dollar amount information to enter into Chrome River or any other questions about Chrome River.
