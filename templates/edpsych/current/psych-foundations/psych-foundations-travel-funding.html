The psychological foundations of education program offers additional funding above and beyond the department award amounts. Eligible graduate students receive funding for student travel, in addition to department level funding, during the academic year.

Award amounts

Up to $500 of travel funds each academic year, defined as the period September 1 to August 31.

Conditions

  • Psychological foundations of education graduate students can request up to $500 of travel funds each academic year, defined as the period September 1 to August 31.
  • Receipts can be submitted for airfare, registration, ground transportation, and hotel. Receipts cannot be subitted for meals or other expenses.
  • A student can make up to two requests per academic year. For example, a student might request $200 for their hotel for conference 1 and $300 for their aifare and registration for conference 2.
  • Funds can only be requested for academic year in which they are incurred.
  • The purpose of the psychological foundations of education travel funding is to support students as researchers and members of their professional community. Therefore, students must be named another on a presentation, poster, roundtable, or other session format. However, we make exceptions, in two circumstances. The first exception is for first-year students, for obvious reasons. The second exception is for senior students (e.g., ABD) who submitted a proposal to a primary conference in their field that was rejected. Because they are close to graduating and entering the job market, attending conferences in their field is important, and they are, therefore, eligible for travel funding once
  • The expectation is that the conference experience will inform the student's research and/or further their professional development, and will be reflected in their U of M activities. For example, it might lead to a presentation or poster at the department's Graduate Student Research Day or college's Research Day, upon returning.

Reimbursement procedure

Upon return, present the following information to Sharon Sawyer within 30 days.

  • Proof of attendance (e.g., conference badge)
  • If you are an author or co-author, a copy of the conference program showing your presentation
  • Original receipts for which you are requesting reimbursement
  • A psychological foundations of education travel request form [PDF]
  • Be sure to include detailed information about your travel: who, what, when, where, and why. (For example, I traveled to Indianapolis, Indiana to present a poster entitled "x" at the national meeting of "abc" society, July 20-25, 20XX.)
  • Submit these materials to Sharon Sawyer (sawye100@umn.edu) within 30 days of the conference.
  • The last day to submit for the current academic year is August 31.

You must submit your reimbursement request through Chrome River within 30 days of the conference. Contact Sharon Sawyer (sawye100@umn.edu) for account and dollar amount information to enter into Chrome River or any other questions about Chrome River.
