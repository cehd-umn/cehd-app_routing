Build your career as a special education researcher and educator

As an M.A. student in the special education program, you'll be trained to find solutions to problems that help individuals with disabilities learn and grow. Many of our special education M.A. students go on to earn their Ph.D. in special education.

Rated #8 in the nation among special education graduate programs by U.S. News and World Report in 2017

Careers

Graduates with their M.A. in special education conduct research, teach in universities, and work in other educational settings in special education.
