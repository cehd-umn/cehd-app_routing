Youngsoon KangPh.D. student

Interests: Profile analysis, educational aspiration of American Indian students, multidimensional factors impacting school violence

Work Youngsoon is currently involved in several projects in the QME program. She works with her advisor, Dr. Ernest Davenport, on a theoretical paper regarding profile analysis, which aims to show how the two statistics, level (average of scores) and pattern (deviance of scores), serve as meaningful predictors under difficult conditions. Youngsoon also works with Dr. Michael Rodriguez and colleagues as a member of the Minnesota Youth Development Research Group (MyDrG). In a 2013 Minnesota Student Survey, she began to focus on American Indian Students in Minnesota, using their youth development construct scores and higher non-response rates, to develop Native American students’ profiles assets and predict their educational aspirations.
