Core courses (12 credits)

Students in the Applied Psychology in Educational and Community Settings (APECS) Minor are required to complete each of the following courses:

EPSY 3301: Introduction to Educational Psychology (3 credits)

EPSY 3132: Psychology of Multiculturalism in Education (3 credits)

EPSY 3264: Basic and Applied Statistics (3 credits)

EPSY 3303: Educational Psychology Undergraduate Research Practicum (3 credits)

Elective courses (3 credits)

Interested in completing credits toward your minor this fall?
