Educational Psychology

Helping people learn and grow

At the department of Educational Psychology, we believe that education is both an art and a science. Our focus: preparing the next generation of researchers and scientist-practitioners with degrees in psychological foundations of education, quantitative methods in education, counseling psychology, school psychology, and special education. In 2018, we were rated #9 in the nation among educational psychology graduate programs by U.S. News & World Report.
