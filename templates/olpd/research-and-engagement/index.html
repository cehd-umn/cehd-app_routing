Engagement

Center for the Study of Leadership in Urban Schools

Dedicated to understanding the challenges facing urban schools and districts, and to helping create solutions that allow our urban youth to succeed.

MasterCard Foundation Learn, Earn, and Save Initiative

A team of faculty and graduate students from the University of Minnesota have embarked on a six-year (2011-2017) evaluation collaboration with The MasterCard Foundation to assess the overall impact of non-formal and formal youth entrepreneurship education initiatives on marginalized youth in Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda.

Minnesota Evaluation Studies Institute (MESI)

A training conference is held each spring for professional evaluators, program directors and staff, community agency staff, students, and others interested in conducting or using evaluations.

Minnesota Principals Academy

Despite the critical and challenging role of the principalship, relatively few principals receive ongoing professional development that enhances their ability to lead schools to high performance. The University of Minnesota sees the ongoing professional development of school leaders as an essential driving force in efforts to improve student learning, close achievement gaps and increase postsecondary completion across the state. The Minnesota Principals Academy provides leaders of Minnesota schools with knowledge, tools and strategies to create schools where all students graduate college and career ready.

Private College Leadership Network

In partnership with the University of Minnesota College of Continuing Education and Minnesota Private College Council (MPCC), the Private College Leadership Network aims to further the professional development of new and experienced leaders of private colleges. A 12-credit certificate program, webinars, and occasional paper series provides opportunities for leaders to network, learn, and collaborate in advancing private colleges.

Urban Leadership Academy (ULA)

In partnership with metro area school districts, the Urban Leadership Academy program aims to further the professional development of new and experienced principals and future principals. Topical conferences, workshops and seminars are offered throughout the year, in addition to an annual summer institute.
