Visiting International Scholar Program

Important Deadlines

  • January 1: application deadline for appointments beginning in the summer semester (May)
  • April 1: application deadline for appointments beginning in the fall semester (September)
  • October 1: application deadline for appointments beginning spring semester (January)

International visiting scholars are one important way in which the University supports international education and develops a global community.

We have a long history of hosting international scholars whose goals are consistent with our research mission and available resources, as well as those of the College of Education and Human Development and the University.

Why OLPD?

Engaging as a visiting scholar requires time, financial investment, and commitment for both the scholar and department. Therefore, it is important to consider to what extent OLPD is a good fit for your scholarly, academic, and professional needs.

We carefully review all visiting scholar applications to determine whether your interests align with the intellectual opportunities we have to offer, as well as our mentoring capacity. As a visiting scholar, you will be expected to share the knowledge and experience stemming from your participation in conferences or professional meetings.

All visiting scholars must have a tenured or tenure track faculty host from OLPD during their entire stay. Please review our website to identify faculty whose work aligns with your interests.

Services

A visiting scholar in OLPD will be assigned the service title of Visiting Scholar without pay. It is important to note that a visiting scholar does not hold an official academic appointment in the Department or University.

As a visiting scholar in OLPD, you:

  • may visit our department for up to one year
  • are provided with a shared office space and access to a computer and printer (if available)
  • are given free reading and borrowing privileges in the University libraries
  • may attend courses on a non-credit basis with instructor approval
  • may use our fax machine to send faxes within the United States (budget and staffing limitations prohibit use of our equipment to make copies, place long distance telephone calls, or obtain assistance from support staff or graduate students)
  • will be provided with access to University housing information (you will be responsible for securing housing arrangements for yourself and housing / education placements for any accompanying family members)

Upon payment of applicable fees, you may:

  • use the services of the International Scholar and Student Services Offices
  • access computer labs
  • purchase photocopying services at the University libraries and computer labs
  • use the University recreational facilities upon payment of fees

Issues to Consider

Currently, visiting scholars must have a completed doctorate degree or equivalent, unless there are compelling reasons to make an exception. Such an exception must be approved by the department chair.

If you are interested in visiting the university to accomplish goals other than research (i.e., serve as a guest lecturer, audit a course, learn about programs and operations, etc.), please contact Alissa Jones, OLPD Administrative Director.

Research Plan

All visiting scholars are required to have a well-developed research plan that focuses on an intellectual area that aligns with the scope and focus of the department’s academic work. The plan is submitted as part of the overall online application. The research plan should articulate what you hope to accomplish during your stay, the specific activities you plan to engage in, and any products you plan to produce as a result of your visit.

Length of Stay

Visiting scholars may visit the Department for up to one academic year. Most OLPD faculty are not available to work with visiting scholars during the summer months. It is important that an early understanding be established about the proposed length of stay. The anticipated length of stay may determine the kind of visa under which the scholar will be eligible to enter the U.S. (For visa information please contact the International Student and Scholar Services office.

Obligations

In addition to submitting an application, a visiting international scholar needs to:

  • make arrangements for housing
  • obtain leave from the home institution
  • be fully funded with financial resources outside the University of Minnesota, and assume financial responsibility for visa processing fees of the University of Minnesota’s International Student and Scholar Services
  • provide health insurance individually, and for any accompanying family members for the duration of the agreed upon time period (all visiting scholars and their dependents are required to enroll in the University of Minnesota Student Health Benefit Plan unless they are covered by another US-based, employer-sponsored health plan. More information on the University of Minnesota Student Health Benefit plan may be obtained by contacting studins@bhs.umn
