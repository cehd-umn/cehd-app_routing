Bio

Joe is both an assistant professor of psychology at Minnesota State University, Mankato and a member of the faculty at Metropolitan State University in St. Paul. Having delivered just shy of 100 course sections, he is an accomplished educator. Classes he has taught include human resource development, organization development, industrial-organizational psychology, research methods, and statistics. Outside the classroom he enjoys cooking, travelling, and spending time with his dog Sophie.
