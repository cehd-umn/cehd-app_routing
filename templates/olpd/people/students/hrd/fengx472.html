Bio

I am a Ph.D. candidate majoring in Human Resource Development. I am currently working as Dr. Ken Bartlett's research assistant, and TA for Project Management class (OLPD 3318) and Doctoral Research Seminar I (OLPD 8011) in the Department of Organizational Leadership, Policy, and Development, University of Minnesota.

Beyond academia, I have experience in recreation project management, club management, and coaching. In my spare time, I like playing badminton, cooking, traveling, and watching documentaries.
