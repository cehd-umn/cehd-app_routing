Bio

As a former public school teacher and longtime college access program employee, educational equity and access to free, quality public education is of the utmost interest. These experiences have guided my research interests and are the purpose for pursuing my doctorate at the U of M. I love teaching OLPD 5005: School and Society where my teacher education students from across the licensure areas learn more about education philosophy, history of education, and education policy. I also enjoy my work supporting the Office of Teacher Education as a Continuous Improvement Fellow in which I work with standards review, advocacy, and connecting with specific teacher education programs.
