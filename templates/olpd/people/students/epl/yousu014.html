Bio

I am a doctoral student at the University of Minnesota studying PK-12 educational leadership. My current research explores the racial, ethnic, and religious identity development of east African immigrants in relation to their education experiences. Prior to joining the doctoral program, I worked in non-profit organizations, conducted program evaluations, and was an academic advisor. Through my desire to live in a more just world, I examine how colonialism, racism, and systemic inequalities impact minoritized populations. My goal is to utilize that understanding to imagine and work toward better future possibilities for my communities.
