Bio

My undergraduate degree was from Cornell University in International Development. Prior to joining Cornell University, I was Head of the Planning, Monitoring, Evaluation and Reporting Unit for District Governance and Community Development, an €80 million program funded by the European Union. I have over 12 years of experience in the development sector in Pakistan working with several national and International NGOs. My key achievements include designing monitoring and evaluation systems such as Tracking Sheets, Statistical Abstracts, field monitoring templates, training feedback reports, and management information systems for data and record keeping. My primary research interest is the evaluation of international projects & programs incorporating the concept of “Living in Harmony with Nature”. After completing my PhD, I am aiming for a Monitoring and Evaluation management position in an international organization—preferably the UN or the World Bank.
