Bio

At the same time as I am pursuing my PhD, I'm also working in international and intercultural education here at the University of Minnesota in the Carlson Global Institute. Through my work supporting faculty leading students abroad, I've become interested in how intercultural learning is included (or not) in courses. As a result, my research will focus on faculty motivation to include intercultural learning in their business courses. For many faculty at the business school, intercultural learning is an innovative approach to teaching and so my research brings together ideas of innovation adoption, motivation, and intercultural learning into one conversation. While my entry point has been study abroad, I'm interested in the current shift (thank you, COVID-19...) away from international mobility and an assumption that intercultural is inherently international. As such, I consider intercultural learning to encompass any intentional engagement with difference—thus my interest in intersectionality.
