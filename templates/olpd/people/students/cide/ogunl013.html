Bio

I am a doctoral student in the Organizational Leadership, Policy, and Development Program with a major focus on Comparative and International Development Education and minor in Program Evaluation. I am broadly interested in critically exploring the development of African universities as sites for nation-building. My dissertation research is a qualitative, comparative case study of Nigerian universities participating in a World Bank funded initiative called “The Africa Centers of Excellence Project”. In this study, I explore the lived experiences of faculty, administrators, and policymakers from multiple institutions as they make meaning of what ‘excellence’ is, and how it should be enacted, within their respective organizations.

My professional background is in higher education administration with a focus on facilitating internationalization projects at both public and private universities based in Texas, USA. I have also taught an Introduction to Project Management course (OLPD 3318) in the College of Education & Human Development at the University of Minnesota-Twin Cities. My goal upon graduation is to work in the field of international development on projects related to strengthening higher education systems in African countries.
