Bio

I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Comparative and International Development Education. Professionally, until October 2017, I was the Collaborating, Learning and Adapting (CLA) advisor with the United States Agency for International Development (USAID) Overseas Missions/Liberia. I have over a decade of experience in project management, capacity development, forging donor strategic partnerships, managing complex donors, civil society, and host country government collaboration efforts. Over the years, I have garnered a wide range of skills, which include facilitation, technical assistance and training, legal and policy research, superb written and oral communications skills, knowledge management, policy analysis, program evaluation, and strategic planning.

As an educator, I have taught courses in global politics, defense, security, and peacebuilding, as well as a course in leadership and governance at the University of Liberia. Before departing post in October 2017, I was a manager of a complex $21 million USAID-funded Liberia Strategic Analysis (LSA) as the Contracting Officer’s Representative (COR), having co-designed the activity for improved program coordination for better development outcomes of USAID’s $840+ million portfolio in Liberia. I am an independent International Development Consultant, primarily working on Collaborating, Learning, and Adapting (CLA) programs in International Development. I currently work as a Restorative Practices professional in my role as Youth & Housing Services Policy Coordinator for Community Mediation & Restorative Services, Inc. (CMRS, Inc.), New Hope, Minnesota.
