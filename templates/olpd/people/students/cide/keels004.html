Bio

I am a Lecturer in Political Science and History for Wenzhou Kean University’s College of Liberal Arts. Prior to working at WKU, I served as the Director of Student Services and Lecturer for the American University of Iraq, Sulaimani. There, I was the primary resource for students for non-academic services at AUIS and a lecturer in Economics. I received my BA from Berea College in Classical Civilizations and German and my MA in Diplomacy and International Commerce from the University of Kentucky Patterson School.
