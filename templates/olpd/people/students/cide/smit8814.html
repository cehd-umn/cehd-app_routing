Bio

I am a fifth-year doctoral student in the Organizational Leadership, Policy, and Development Program with an emphasis on Comparative and International Development Education. With a background in K-12 teaching, I taught for two years in Casablanca, Morocco, and nearly three years in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates (UAE). I am currently collecting data and writing my dissertation, which focuses on diversity and migration through teacher attrition for African American K-12 educators who left the US K-12 system for occupations in education in the UAE.

At UMN, I have taught various courses in the Department of Curriculum and Instruction (CI 3212, 5283, 3283, and 3211) and OLPD (5005, and 5000). I am also the creator and host of the podcast (A)Broad in Education where I (un)pack the international suitcase through interviews with EDpats (expatriates working in education in countries outside of their own).
