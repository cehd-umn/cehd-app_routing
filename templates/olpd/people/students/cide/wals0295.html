Bio

I am a science teacher and coordinate the Global Scholar Program at Edina High School in Minnesota. My passion for global and international education was sparked by my experience as a Fulbright Fellow in Morocco in 2019, where I researched the language of instruction in science education. Another passion of mine is student travel, in particular, environmental conservation research and connecting students to Indigenous communities with their fieldwork. I became a National Geographic Certified Educator in 2019 and continue to be involved with their education initiatives, including becoming a course mentor for new teachers undergoing the certification process. In my free time, I love being outdoors in any way including hiking, diving, camping, etc.
