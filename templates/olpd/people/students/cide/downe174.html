Bio

I am curious about the overlap between universities and the public good in the global context, and the potential public and private benefits of higher education in the building (or rebuilding) of a nation. Based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, I am specifically interested in the Middle East context, including the education to employment gap, emergency education, and intercultural education.

At present, I serve as the Assistant Dean of Students and Director of the Career Development Center at New York University Abu Dhabi. Having worked in higher education on three continents, I aim to connect people with each other and with opportunities, build capacity on teams, and lower barriers to access when it comes to options. In the past, I have presented at local, regional, and international conferences about contextualized career advising, data-driven decision making, regionally relevant recruitment, and the advantage of global mobility.
