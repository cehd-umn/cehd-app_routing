Bio

I grew up in a first-generation Chinese-American home in the suburbs of Milwaukee developing my love for food, my family, and the Midwest. I currently work as an Academic Advisor in the College of Liberal Arts. When not doing school or work, I am active in the ultimate frisbee community and enjoy the outdoors and travelling.
