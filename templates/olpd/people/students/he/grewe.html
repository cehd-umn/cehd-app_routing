Bio

I have worked in higher education since 2007 and am currently the Director of Campus Life at Augsburg University. I have worked in a variety of functional areas, including orientation, student activities, LGBTQIA+ equity, veteran and military affairs, commuter student services, co-curricular assessment, and leadership development, among others. I have also been involved in professional associations, including serving as President of the Minnesota College Professionals Association and as Co-Chair of the Minnesota GLBTA Campus Alliance. I am also an adjunct faculty member at Augsburg University, teaching both in the Master of Social Work program and in the Leadership Studies Minor.
