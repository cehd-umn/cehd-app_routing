Bio

I am an international leadership educator who specializes in working across academic and co-curricular programs. My work is centered in empowering students to develop leadership skills for positive social change through experiential learning. I completed my Masters in Higher Education from the University of Maryland-College Park with an emphasis on Undergraduate Student Leadership Development and hold a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from James Madison University.

I currently work at the University of Minnesota-Twin Cities in roles as a faculty, staff, and student. In my full-time role I currently serve as the Assistant Director for Leadership & Training within Orientation & Transition Experiences (OTE) at the University of Minnesota-Twin Cities. I hold teaching appointments with the Office of Undergraduate Education, Leadership Minor, and Learning Abroad Center. The courses I am currently teaching are OUE3310: Leadership Development for University Student Leaders and LEAD4481: Leadership and Social Change in Ireland.

Previously to moving to Minnesota, I spent five years working abroad across a variety of contexts. This included working as the Assistant Director for Student Engagement for Hamad bin Khalifa University in Qatar, a Consultant for Student Engagement at the University of Auckland, and a Residence Director on Semester at Sea’s Spring 2017 voyage. I grew up on Long Island, New York but currently live in Saint Paul, Minnesota with my partner, daughter (Ruby) and two dogs. I enjoy playing basketball, cooking, camping, road trips, traveling whenever possible, and exploring all that the Twin Cities have to offer!
