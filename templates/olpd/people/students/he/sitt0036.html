Bio

I am interested in higher education as an environment for personal, social, and civic development; specifically for domestic, or host, students to develop global competency and global citizenship. I aim to research domestic and international student friendship; what environments lead to the most successful interactions, and if global competency is increased in domestic students through these interactions.

I have enjoyed gaining experience in various corners of higher education including: residence life, first year programs, new international student programs, graduate programs, and now career services for undergraduate international students.

I am a member of the National Career Development Association, and member of the International Student Services Committee within the group. In 2019 I initiated the International Students Knowledge Community within the Hire Big 10+ Career Consortium. At the University of Minnesota, I am a board member of the Career Development Network, a group of student services staff who support student career development in our work on campus.
