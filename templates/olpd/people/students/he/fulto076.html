Bio

I have more than a decade of experience in higher education, primarily in various Student Affairs roles in addition to research. My dissertation research focuses on Black doctoral student mothers. I am a qualified administrator of the Intercultural Development Inventory who writes, coaches, consults, and facilitates on topics related to race, culture, class, and gender. My publications explore the intersections of race, motherhood, leadership, and higher education. I live in Minneapolis with my partner and our three children.
