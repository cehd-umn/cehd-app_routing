Bio

I am a Hmong American higher education scholar and practitioner with over 6 years of professional experience in student and academic affairs. I am currently the Peer Tutoring Coordinator for SMART Learning Commons at the U. My main areas of research focus on the access and equity of student engagement practices, the role of family in college student academic success, and collecting/understanding the lived experiences of college students at all types of institution types. Additionally, I have taught the following courses at the University of Minnesota: OLPD 1302: Personal Leadership at the U, LASk 1001: Mastering Skills for College Success, and CLA 1005: Intro to Liberal Arts Learning.
