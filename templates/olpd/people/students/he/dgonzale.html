Bio

I am a social justice advocate that strives to advance higher education, specifically for our most marginalized students, staff, and faculty. My research interests are at the intersection of education policy and student access barriers for first-generation students, students of color, and LGBTQIA+ students. I am also interested in strategies that foster the retention and advancement of staff/faculty with marginalized identities (BIPOC & LGBTQIA+ members) in their field of work.

I began the Ph.D. program with eight years of experience within higher education. Since completing my graduate degree in 2012 and leaving my home state of New York, I have worked within student housing across three varying universities. Currently, I am a Coordinator of Residential Life here at the University of Minnesota. Outside of my role, I’ve held leadership positions with the Association of Colleges and University Housing Officers – International (ACUHO-I), as well as regional and state level housing associations. In 2020, I was selected by the ACUHO-I Executive Board to serve on the Anti-Racism Task Force, "which is charged with examining how campus housing and residence life professionals can contribute to the disruption, if not dismantling, of individual biases and systemic oppression that exists on college and university campuses." "

My work is grounded in my lived experiences, identities, and passion for learning. As a person who holds multiple marginalized identities, combined with being an educator by trade, I intend to be a part of the change that will continue to expand access and opportunities across all levels of the system.
