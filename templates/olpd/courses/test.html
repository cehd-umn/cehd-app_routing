2013 Student Research Conference

Saturday, April 20, 2013
9:00 am to 3:30 pm
Continuing Education and Conference Center

Purpose

The goal of the conference was to highlight both undergraduate and graduate student work related to the latest research findings, best practices, and theoretical/conceptual developments within our department while replicating what is encountered in professional association conferences. This conference was made possible through a donation from the Almond A. Clark Endowment Fund.
