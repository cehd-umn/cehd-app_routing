OLPD Ph.D.
Education Policy and Leadership


Educational Policy and Leadership (EPL) will not be admitting students to this full-time Ph.D. program for 2021-22.

The Executive PhD Cohort Program is the only EPL program which will be admitting new doctoral students for a Fall 2021 start. EPL will also be admitting students to its MA program and MEd program for matriculation next fall.

Admission requirements

Degree: Master's degree or equivalent

GPA: Undergraduate 3.0; Graduate 3.5

Note: GRE scores have been waived as an admission requirement for programs beginning in Fall 2021.

  • Applicants should not submit GRE scores, as they will not be considered in the review process.

TOEFL/IELTS Scores (Not required for U.S. students):

  • TOEFL: Internet based = 79 or above
    (21 writing/19 reading)
  • IELTS = 6.5

Experience (International educators only): 3 years experience in international education (preferred)

Readmission

If a graduate student in an OLPD program has become inactive they must follow the readmission procedures.
