OLPD 5696
Graduate Internship: Human Resource Development

For M.A., M.Ed., and Certificate Students in Human Resource Development and Adult Education

The purpose of this graduate internship is to provide students an opportunity to engage in real-life human resource development (HRD) experiences under the supervision of HRD practitioners from industry, (for-profit or not-for-profit business), education, consulting, and government. While the internship may be job related and conducted at the student's place of employment, the activities should be above and beyond normal job responsibilities. Students are encouraged to limit activities to those that can be achieved within a semester. HRD activities include training and development, organization development, career planning and development, and adult education.

Internship process and position opportunities.

Training and development (T&D)

Activities include conducting needs assessments, designing training programs, developing training materials, delivering training, and training evaluation.

Organization development (OD)

Activities include significant participation in any phase of OD practice/intervention designed to enhance capacity at the individual, group, or organizational level. These practices include team building, coaching, assessment of organization culture (including that which occurs as a result of mergers and acquisitions), engagement in diversity initiatives, or any of the activities related to the attached list of OD practices/interventions. The department encourages host sites to pay student interns; however, there may be times when a non-paid internship is appropriate.

Career development and planning (CPD)

Activities include working with staff HRD professionals to develop processes and materials to aid in career development discussions with employees or clients. This could include documenting and writing descriptions of typical career path moves made by past employees, and helping to assemble career resource documents for use by current employees. It could also involve work on informational sessions regarding career possibilities designed for both current and prospective employees.

Adult education (ADED)

Activities include field experiences in public school, adult education programs, community education settings, non-profit sponsored adult education programs, workforce development settings, and similar environments where and intern is involved in teaching/tutoring in an adult education activity, designing a new program for adult learners, implementing a program in adult education setting, or evaluate a current adult education program. Adult Education majors must work directly with the adult education faculty for this internship and may enroll in OLPD 5296—Field Experience when there is agreement on the activity.
