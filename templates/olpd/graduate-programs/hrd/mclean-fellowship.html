McLean Fellowship


For M.A., M.Ed., and Certificate Students in Human Resource Development and Adult Education

This fellowship is named for professor emeritus Gary McLean in recognition of his profound impact on international HRD, as well as his many contributions to the HRD and Adult Education programs at the University of Minnesota over his 38 year career. Gifts from current and former students have funded this fellowship to recognize and support students in their study and research related to international HRD. A unique aspect of the fellowship is that awardees invite Professor McLean to lunch for a chance to share research interests and projects.
