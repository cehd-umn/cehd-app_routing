About CIDE at Minnesota

We are a community of scholars who examine education from global, interdisciplinary, and comparative perspectives. We look at how culture, politics, and economics influence educational philosophies and infrastructures. We care deeply about gender relations, human rights, international relations, migration, social justice, equity, intercultural competence. We come from a wide range of academic backgrounds and hail from countries across the globe.

Our programs prepare students to become active scholars, policy analysts, and educators. They provide intensive, hands-on experience conducting policy-relevant research. Faculty and students in our program work closely together on research and development projects, which often involve fieldwork in diverse countries and over multiple years.
