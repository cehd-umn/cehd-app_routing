M.A. curriculum
Multicultural College Teaching and Learning

M.A. students choose between Plan A and Plan B options. Plan A students register for thesis credits, write a master's thesis, and meet with their committee members for an oral defense of the thesis. Plan B students write a final examination paper that they submit to their committee members for review and approval.

Plan A | Plan B

Plan A (34 credits)

The Plan A master's requires a minimum of 34 credits. Students must complete a minimum of 15 credits in the major field (including practicum and internship), a minimum of 6 elective credits outside, one additional 3 credit research methods course, as well as 10 thesis credits and a master's thesis.

Required core courses (15 credits minimum)

  • CI 5106—Multicultural Teaching and Learning in Diverse College Contexts (3 cr)
  • CI 5116—Action Research Methods to Improve College Teaching and Learning (3 cr)
  • OLPD 5712—Multicultural Theories of College Student Development Applied to Teaching and Learning (3 cr)
  • OLPD 5796—Supervised Practicum in Multicultural Postsecondary Teaching and Learning (3 cr)
  • OLPD 8796—Supervised Internship in Postsecondary Teaching and Learning (3 cr required, additional credits may be taken with adviser approval)
