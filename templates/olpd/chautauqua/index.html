Keynote: Dr. Kathi Tunheim

Dr. Kathi Tunheim is currently serving as the Vice-President for Mission, Strategy and Innovation at Gustavus Adolphus College in St. Peter. MN. She is also the Acting Director of Human Resources. She founded three distinguished organizations at Gustavus: Gustavus Women in Leadership, Gustavus Mentoring Program and the Gustie Entrepreneur Cup. In 2008 Kathi earned her Ph.D. from the University of Minnesota in Human Resource Development. She currently serves on the board of Lutheran World Relief-IMA World Health and Thrivent Financials’ Member Network in the Central Region. Kathi is also serving as the President of Trinity Lutheran Church in Long Lake/Orono. Kathi was recently honored to receive the Distinguished Alumni award from the University of Minnesota in the College of Education and Human Development.



Panelist: Susan R. Madsen

Professor Susan R. Madsen is considered one of the top global scholars and thought leaders on the topic of women’s leadership, has authored or edited six books, and has published hundreds of articles, chapters, and reports. Her research has been cited in the U.S. News and World Report, The Atlantic, The New York Times, Parenting Magazine, Chronicle of Higher Education, and The Washington Post. She is also a regular contributor to Forbes and other local and state newspapers. Dr. Susan R. Madsen is the Inaugural Karen Haight Huntsman Endowed Professor of Leadership in the Jon M. Huntsman School of Business at Utah State University and serves on many nonprofit and community boards. Her passion is to strengthen the impact of girls and women worldwide.



Panelist: Dr. Michael P. Leimbach

As Vice President of Global Research and Development for Wilson Learning Worldwide, Michael Leimbach is responsible for the design and development of Wilson Learning’s learning and development solutions. In addition, Dr. Leimbach is past Editor of Advances in Developing Human Resources and is a member of ISO Technical Committee on Quality Standards for Learning Services. He has published over 100 articles and is a contributing author for six books.



Panelist: Dr. Stephanie Sisco

Stephanie Sisco is a Post-Doctoral Associate at the University of Minnesota–Twin Cities, and Assistant Editor of New Horizons in Adult Education & Human Resource Development. She received her B.A. in Communication and History from the University of California – Davis, M.S.Ed. in Adult and Higher Education from Northern Illinois University, and Ph.D. in Organizational, Leadership, Policy, and Development from the University of Minnesota – Twin Cities. Her research seeks to understand how social issues appear at work and influence the learning, development, and advancement of Black, Indigenous, and People of Color (BIPOC). Dr. Sisco is a recipient of the 2017 Academy of Human Resource Development Cutting Edge Award, and she has also worked with numerous workforce development programs throughout the Twin Cities, including Avivo, Twin Cities RISE!, Tree Trust, and EMERGE.



Panelist: Kibum Noh

Kibum Noh is a doctoral candidate in HRD at the University of Minnesota. His research is on workplace diversity, technology, and organizational changes. His teaching includes Microeconomics for Master’s of HR students and Training & Development for undergraduate students. He holds a Master's in Finance from the University of Illinois and HRIR from the University of Minnesota, prior to that he worked at Hyundai and Korea Economic Research Institute.
