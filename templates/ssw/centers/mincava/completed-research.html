Completed research projects

The longer-term effects of civil legal services on battered women

Team
Carolyn Copps Hartley (PI; University of Iowa) and Lynette M. Renner

Funder
National Institute of Justice

Project Period
01/01/2011-03/30/2016

Objectives

  • Conduct a longer-term outcome study (safety, psychological well-being, and economic self-sufficiency) of women who have experienced IPV and are receiving civil legal services.
  • Disseminate study findings to practitioners and policy makers working to reduce the incidence and impact of domestic violence on victims.

Status
Data analyses and dissemination activities are ongoing.


Exploring mental and behavioral health outcomes among children exposed to intimate partner violence

Team
Lynette M. Renner (University PI), Angela Lewis-Dmello (Community PI; Domestic Abuse Project), Cari Jo Clark (co-Investigator), Hannah Michel (project coordinator)

Funder
UMN, Clinical and Translational Science Institute

Project period
07/01/2014-06/30/2016

Objectives

  • Investigate how exposure to a 12-week parent and child psychoeducational intervention changes children’s mental and behavioral health, the quality of the parent-child relationship and women’s perceptions of parenting.

Status
Data analyses and dissemination activities are ongoing.

How safe are LGBTQ youth in Minnesota schools? An examination of school climate and bullying

Team
Colleen Fisher (PI) and Lynette M. Renner (co-PI)

Objectives

  • Examine youth’s perceptions of school climate for LGBTQ youth in Minnesota
  • Examine the prevalence of bullying of LGBTQ youth in school settings in Minnesota
  • Identify risk and protective factors associated with school-based LGBTQ bullying
  • Assess the use of LGBTQ-focused resources and supports in Minnesota schools

Status
This project was one of 29 research studies conducted at the 2014 Minnesota State Fair as part of the University’s Driven to Discover initiative. A total of 788 high-school students from Minnesota participated in the online survey. Data analyses and dissemination activities are ongoing.

Microaggressions, bullying & bystander behavior: Investigating LGBTQ student wellbeing in Minnesota

Team
Team: Lynette M. Renner (PI) and Colleen Fisher (co-PI)

Objectives

  • Examine the prevalence of microaggressions and bullying toward LGBTQ youth and young adults in educational settings (i.e., high schools and college campuses) in Minnesota
  • Investigate the consequences of microaggressions and bullying toward LGBTQ youth/young adults
  • Examine the extent to which experienced and observed mircoaggressions/bullying affect both the victims and bystanders, respectively
  • Assess the extent to which bystanders have intervened when observing LGBTQ microaggressions and bullying in educational settings, how they have intervened, and the reasons for intervening or not

Status
This project was one of 41 research studies conducted at the 2015 Minnesota State Fair as part of the University’s Driven to Discover initiative. A total of 956 high-school and college students from Minnesota participated in the online survey. Data analyses and dissemination activities are ongoing.
