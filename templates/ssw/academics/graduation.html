Graduation

There are two graduation/recognition ceremonies in May for recent graduates and students who will complete all degree requirements by December 2018: the college's Commencement ceremony and the School of Social Work's recognition ceremony. Students may opt to participate the CEHD Commencement ceremony or the Social Work Student Recognition Ceremony, or both. RSVPs for CEHD Commencement and SSW Recognition Ceremony must be submitted separately.

CEHD Commencement

The CEHD graduate student commencement ceremony will be held Wednesday, May 8, 2019, at 3M Arena at Mariucci. You must RSVP for this ceremony online. RSVP and other pertinent information.
