What is Youth Studies?

  • Interdisciplinary studies of youth: anthropological, political, sociological, phenomenological, biopsychological, and historical approaches
  • Identify and interrogate ideas and ask increasingly complicated questions about young people and what is known about them
  • Engage in critical reflection on your own experiences of being a young person
  • Learn about the everyday lives of youth
  • Leave prepared to design and engage in a range of youthwork practices and professional space
