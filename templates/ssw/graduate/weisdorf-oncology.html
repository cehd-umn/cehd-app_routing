Weisdorf Oncology Social Work Fellowship

In 2019-2020, this fellowship will provide financial support for one (1) graduate social work student who is interested in pursuing a career that focuses on oncology social work practice in an inpatient health care setting.

This one-time fellowship provides an $8,000 award and a $1,000 stipend for travel expenses to attend the Association of Oncology Social Work annual conference. The award and travel stipend are offered during the time the student is enrolled in a concentration field placement in oncology social work at University of Minnesota Health. This fellowship is available only for concurrent field placements during the academic year (fall and spring semesters; summer fellowships are not available).

Preference is given to a student who is studying on a full-time basis.

Eligibility

The student must be in either the Health, Disability and Aging (HDA) or Clinical Mental Health (CMH) concentration, and must complete the requirements of the Oncology Social Work Fellowship curriculum (see MSW student handbook). While the student may choose either concentration, first priority in selection will go to a student in the HDA concentration.

The student must be placed at, and accept, a concentration internship (SW 8020 or SW 8030) at University of Minnesota Health, which provides training in inpatient oncology social work.

Application

All students who are applying for this fellowship should also complete the 2019-2020 Social Work Student Financial Aid Form.

Special requirements

The student who receives this award must complete the HDA or CMH concentration requirements as outlined in the MSW handbook, and must be placed in a concentration field placement (SW 8020 – Field Practicum II or SW 8030 – Advanced Standing Field Practicum) at University of Minnesota Health.

There is only one approved field placement site for this fellowship: University of Minnesota Health (Inpatient Oncology Service).

IMPORTANT NOTE: Because this award requires an inpatient placement at University of Minnesota Health, the application process does not occur until AFTER the field matching process in May.
