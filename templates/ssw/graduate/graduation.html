Graduation

Students who will complete all degree requirements by December 2019 may participate in two graduation/recognition ceremonies in May. The CEHD Commencement ceremony and the School of Social Work's recognition ceremony. Students may opt to participate in either ceremony.

CEHD commencement

The CEHD graduate student commencement ceremony will be held Wednesday, May 8, 2019, at 3M Arena at Mariucci. No tickets are necessary because the arena has very ample seating. Seating is on a first-come basis.

Social Work Recognition Ceremony for PhD and MSW Students

The School of Social Work will host the Social Work Student Recognition Ceremony on Sunday, May 5, 2019, at 4:00 p.m. The ceremony will be held at the Ted Mann Concert Hall on the West Bank campus. An RSVP form will be posted mid-January 2019.

About the recognition ceremony

MSW and PhD degree candidates who plan to graduate by December 2019 are honored at this recognition ceremony. While this is not an official University commencement event, there is a presentation of degree candidates. A light reception for degree candidates and their guests is held immediately afterward in the lobby of the Ted Mann Concert Hall. The ceremony and reception are scheduled from 4–6 p.m.

Most students wear caps, gowns, and degree hoods at the ceremony. Caps and gowns may be obtained at the University Bookstore in Coffman Memorial Union or at St. Paul Student Center, and online. For more information, please visit the Bookstore's website.
