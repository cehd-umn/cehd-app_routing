Positions at the School of Social Work

The School of Social Work is looking for community faculty members to teach the Fall 2019 and Spring 2020 semester courses listed below. Applicants must be available to teach the days and times listed for the class. Courses meet weekly, unless noted.

For more information about position requirements and how to apply, select "External Applicants" on the University of Minnesota employment web site and enter 330643 in the "Search Jobs" box.
