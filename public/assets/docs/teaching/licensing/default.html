Initial License in Minnesota

If you are in an M.Ed. Initial Licensure Program (ILP) you will be applying for an initial license (even if you hold a MN substitute and/or restricted license), and should follow these instructions:

Checklist Before Applying for License

  1. Review fingerprint card instructions. [PDF]
    • You will need to be fingerprinted even if you already completed the process for student teaching.
    • If you hold a limited, substitute, or regular Minnesota teaching license, you do not need to be fingerprinted again.
    • After your prints have been taken, include the Minnesota Department of Education (MDE) fingerprint card in your teaching license application folder.
  2. Pass all remaining Minnesota Teacher Licensure Examinations (MTLE)
  3. Sign a Transcript Release form. [PDF]
  4. Check your APAS report on One Stop to make sure you have completed all license requirements and that your grades have been posted.

Applying for and Submitting a Minnesota Teaching License Application

After your final grades have been posted and you have completed the checklist items above, follow these steps to apply for your Minnesota teaching license:

First-time applicants: Instructions for completing the Minnesota teaching license application. [PDF]

Applicants who already hold a Minnesota teaching license: Instructions for completing the Minnesota teaching license application. [PDF]

After Submitting Your Application

After completing all the steps in applying for licensure, you will receive an email from OTE that you have been recommended to MDE for licensure. Your license will be mailed to you by MDE within 8-12 weeks.

Additional License

If you are in an additional license program and already hold a full-time teaching license in Minnesota, follow these steps:

Once you have completed the course requirements, state mandated testing requirements (if needed), and all grades are posted to your transcript you can proceed with your application for the additional license.

Your application packet must include the following:

  • The MDE Application for an Addition to a Minnesota Education License. This can be downloaded at the MN Department of Education, and click on "Application to Add a Field to an Existing Minnesota License."
  • On page 2 of the MDE application for an Addition to a Minnesota Education License, please complete the Student Teaching/Practicum/Internship Experience section.
  • The Conduct Review Statement. This form is  part of the Application listed above.
  • A copy of your current Minnesota teaching license.
  • A check made payable to the “Commissioner of Education”. The fee is listed on your application form.
  • Official transcripts, in the sealed envelope, from other colleges or universities, if you have taken coursework towards the added license following admission to the license program. A University of Minnesota transcript is not required.
  • Sign a Transcript Release form. [PDF]
  • Complete the CEHD Exit Survey sent to your U of M email.
  • Official MTLE test score results sheet, if you did not have them reported to the University of Minnesota. We will return your official score report to you.

Mail your complete application packet to:

Office of Teacher Education - Attn: Pam Matti
110 Wulling Hall
86 Pleasant St. SE
Minneapolis, MN 55455

If you have further questions, please contact OTE at ote@umn.edu.

After Submitting Your Application

Most complete application packets are reviewed, signed, and mailed to the Minnesota Department of Education within two weeks. This timeline maybe extended during the busy summer season. The Minnesota Department of Education (MDE) will typically take from 8-12 weeks for processing of your license. Please follow the processing of your license through the MDE’s web site.

Other Licenses

Special instructions for Adult Basic Education, Parent Education, and School Counseling initial licensure (first-time license) candidates:

Checklist Before Applying for License

  1. Submit your fingerprinting using the MDE fingerprint card. Fingerprint cards are available from Minnesota Department of Education (MDE) Personnel Licensing, 1500 Highway 36 West, Roseville, MN 55113; 651-582-8691.
  2. Adult Basic Education and Parent Education applicants: Take the Minnesota NES Essential Academic Skills tests (Reading, Writing and Mathematics) and have the scores reported to the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities.
  3. School Counseling applicants: Your MA degree must be posted to your transcript prior to completing your Application for License via the MDE.
  4. All applicants: If you have taken coursework towards licensure that is not listed on your license program contract/checklist or if you have taken coursework at another institution that you wish to use towards the license, be sure to file the appropriate Academic Policy Petition form with your faculty adviser and with the Office of Teacher Education (OTE) prior to applying for your license.

Applying for and Submitting a Minnesota Teaching License Application

After your final grades have been posted (all candidates), your MA degree has been posted (School Counseling candidates only), and you have completed the checklist items above, follow the steps below to apply for your Minnesota teaching license:

  1. Complete the Minnesota teaching license application.
  2. Print the following two items: MDE License Application Cover Page and Verification of Completion of a State-Approved Licensure Program form (these forms are generated after you complete the online application for license with the MDE). After printing the forms, complete the section Student Teaching/Practicum/Internship Experience on the Verification of Completion form.
  3. Complete, print, and sign the Transcript Release form. [PDF] Electronic signatures are not accepted.
  4. Adult Basic Education and Parent Education: Complete the CEHD Exit Survey sent to your U of M email account.
  5. Submit the following materials to the applicable address below:
    • MDE License Application Cover Page
    • Verification of Completion of a State-Approved Licensure Program form
    • Completed fingerprint card
    • Signed Transcript Release form

License Outside of Minnesota

The Office of Teacher Education (OTE) highly recommends that you apply for the Minnesota teaching license as soon as you complete the program (including all licensure requirements:  coursework, student teaching and MTLE tests) even if you don't want to teach right away or if you plan to teach in another state.

If you choose to apply for the Minnesota license later on, you will have to complete all of the requirements at the time of application. These requirements may be more extensive than the ones in place right now.

Apply for the teaching license through the educator licensing board of the state in which you wish to teach. Each state has a unique set of instructions and forms, which you will complete for that state's teacher license.

Complete the application form and provide all requested materials. Note that many states require the use of their own fingerprint card and will conduct their own criminal background check. States often require you to pass their specific licensure examinations (in addition to MTLE exams).

Many states will require an Institutional Verification Form. If the state requires this form, they will provide it as part of their application process. Please verify that all of your Minnesota licensure requirements are completed (coursework, student teaching, and MTLE tests) before submitting an institutional verification form to CEHD Student Services. Applying for your Minnesota license concurrently with your out-of-state license will expedite the process.

You will need to submit this form to:

Office of Teacher Education
110 Wulling Hall
86 Pleasant St. SE
Minneapolis, MN 55455

OTE will complete the verification form and mail it to that state’s licensing board or directly to student's address listed on the form.
