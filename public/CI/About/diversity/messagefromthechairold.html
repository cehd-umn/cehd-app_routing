Message from the Chair: An Invitation to the C&I Community


I am writing to express sorrow over the lethal shooting of Philando Castile by a police officer.  This tragedy in our own backyard in the wake of the shooting of two Muslim men, the lethal shooting of Alton Sterling by a police officer, the killing of 49 people at the Pulse bar in Orlando, and the fatal shooting of five police officers at the end of a peaceful protest in Dallas, makes it hard for many of us to get past an overwhelming feeling of despair.

One way to support each other and foster a culture of conversation across our shared and particular concerns is by sharing responses, critiques, questions, feelings, and suggestions. In this vein, we invite you—our students, faculty, and staff—to contribute poems, art, spoken word, commentary, or other forms of response and invite you to post on our Facebook page.

As students of education, teacher educators, and community-engaged researchers, we must overcome despair with renewed determination to foster educational practices that lead to a more just and democratic society. This mission of the Department of Curriculum and Instruction is ever more critical in the face of a legacy of racism and systems of oppression that have included educational institutions.  Working on equity in education is one way that we can take action against violence and develop publics with the capacity to be empathic, thoughtful, questioning, critical, and community-engaged. I am confident that the C&I community has the knowledge and conviction to work together toward these goals.

Sincerely,

Cynthia Lewis
Chair, Department of Curriculum and Instruction
