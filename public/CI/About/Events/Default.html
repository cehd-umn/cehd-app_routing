Emma Birkmaier Critical Literacy and Urban Education Speaker Series

The Critical Literacy and Urban Education Speaker Series is dedicated to bringing leading national and local speakers to address the social and political contexts that shape the literacy practices of youth in urban settings and the potential for transformative literacy learning in school. This series is sponsored by the Emma Birkmaier Professorship in Educational Leadership.

Naomi C. Chase Lecture in Children's Literature

The Chase Lecture in Children’s Literature is an annual event hosted by the Department of Curriculum and Instruction. The lecture commemorates Professor Naomi C. Chase who taught at the University between 1950 and 1976 and was particularly interested in helping children develop as writers. Invited speakers are authors or illustrators recognized for making a significant contribution to the field of children’s literature. Since 1980 when it was established, the event has drawn such award-winning authors as Marguerite Henry, Myra Cohn Livingstone, Katherine Paterson, Paul Fleischman, Lois Lowry, James Cross Giblin, Sy Montgomery and Marilyn Nelson. The 2014 Event will feature Jane Yolen.

The Chase Lecture is free and open to the public. It is co-sponsored by the Department of Curriculum and Instruction, the College of Education and Human Development, the University of Minnesota’s Children’s Literature Research collection, home of the Kerlan, and Kerlan Friends.

To learn more about C&I's Graduate Student Research Day, please visit the Curriculum & Instruction Graduate Student Association (CIGSA) website.

C&I Pro-seminars

Your graduate program is preparing you to be successful in an academic career (and a distinguished one at that, right?), going beyond the term papers and class projects of yore. With so much to accomplish in so short a time, pro-seminars can aid in demystifying some of the more enigmatic elements of that transition from “graduate work” to “work work”.

Pro-seminars are short, content-rich seminars that C&I faculty have developed to help graduate students navigate the sometimes murky ocean of academia (e.g. conference-going, dissertations, job searches, etc).

Recent topics have included:

  • Presenting at conferences: Why? How to choose? How to prepare?
  • Non-academic research careers and academic non-R-1 careers
  • PhD Program Planning: What courses to take and when to take them

You can find videos of our recent events on the PhD Current Student Resources page.

Diversity Dialogues

Diversity Dialogues are monthly gatherings focused on education, difference, and social justice.  Each Dialogue features a presentation by a local or visiting scholar, followed by questions and conversation. Diversity Dialogues are sponsored by the Diversity Committee of the Department of Curriculum & Instruction. These events are open to the public.

Recent events include:

  • Vershawn Ashanti Young: Making Black Lives Matter in Online Spaces
  • Jennie Diaz: Math of All? The Paradox of Inlcusion and Inequality in Elementary School Mathematics

The States of Southeast Asian American Studies Conference

October 2-3, 2014

Suggestive of shifting politics and conditions, this conference considers the now established field of Southeast Asian American studies. As a research site, Southeast Asian American studies encompasses multiple sociopolitical formations that intersect with war, immigration, race and identity. At the same time, the conditions that “brought the field into being” have shifted in light of scholarship in social sciences, education, the humanities, and cultural studies. Last, but certainly not least, the notion of “stating” where the field is and why it remains relevant – in terms of emergent scholarship and interdisciplinary engagement – foregrounds this Yconference, which considers the past, present, and future of Southeast Asian American Studies.

Visit the conference website for more details.

STEM Seminar Series

The STEM Seminar Series, sponsored by the STEM Education Center, features guest lectures by some of the most influential researchers in the field of STEM Education. The series attempts to address timely and relevant research questions and innovations specific to the field. These events occur monthly and are open to the public.
