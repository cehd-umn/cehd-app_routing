NCTE Assembly for Research Mid-Winter Conference

"Teaching and Researching Across Color Lines: Literacies, Pedagogies, and the Politics of Difference"

Workshops

Donaldo Macedo
Literacy for Stupidification

In this workshop we will examine the failure of literacy as it is practiced and understood by those "enlightened" and most charged with its promotion. How do educators find themselves complying with a literacy that systematically undercuts peoples' abilities to be engaged in the real world - a world of social, political, and historical complexity? How is it that a focus on language, far from leading to enabling possibilities, becomes instead a tool for social reproduction and oppression? What literacies are needed to thoroughly embrace the world and stem the dulling of human sensibility?

If possible, please read the following article in preparation for this preconference workshop:

Macedo, D. (1993). Literacy for stupidification: The pedagogy of big lies. Harvard Educational review, 63(2), 183-206.

Alice McIntyre
From Dialogue to Action: Participatory Research with Urban Youth

In this workshop, I discuss a participatory action research (PAR) project I engaged in for four years in an urban community in Bridgeport, CT. This workshop focuses on a group of young adolescents of Color who were the primary participants in a collaborative project of reflection and action. The workshop will include a brief slide show that the young people crafted for a number of presentations they gave to funders, university personnel, politicians, and educators. Their work reveals “a” powerful way of engaging universities and urban communities in collaborative processes of individual and collective change.
