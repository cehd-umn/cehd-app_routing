Cooperating teachers

Are you interested in working with a teacher candidate?

Cooperating teachers mentor our student teachers in our initial license programs.

A student teacher brings many benefits to your classroom including:

  • reduction in the student/teacher ratio
  • enhanced classroom management
  • increased student participation and engagement
  • increased instructional options for all students

Our teaching candidates are highly qualified

Because our initial licensure programs are offered at the master's level our teacher candidates bring the wealth ofcontent knowledge acquired during the completion of their undergraduate degrees.

For more information on working with student teachers, please visit the Educator Development and Research Center (EDRC)'s section on cooperating teachers.
