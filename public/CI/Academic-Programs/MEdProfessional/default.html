M.Ed. Professional Studies Programs

The Master of Education Professional Studies programs are for licensed teachers and other professionals interested in gaining advanced knowledge or leadership skills in their field.

Schools and districts interested in partnering to offer cohort-based professional studies M.Ed programs, please contact the Chair or a faculty member in the program of interest. 

How is an M.Ed. different from an M.A.?

An M.A. in our department is typically for students that want to improve their professional practice, along with study theory, develop research skills, and possibly prepare for a doctoral degree program. The M.Ed. Professional Studies programs are more practitioner-focused. The M.Ed. emphasizes applied coursework and usually requires a field-based experience. M.Ed. Professional Studies applicants are often:

  • teachers or other education professionals that have an initial license, but not a master's degree.
  • education professionals that do not require an initial license, but would like to earn a master's degree.

Transfer Credits

You may transfer a maximum of 12 graduate semester credits to the M.Ed. program. Credits may include any combination of the following coursework:

  • Graduate semester credits from fully accredited institutions other than the University of Minnesota
  • Credits completed at the University of Minnesota before admission to the M.Ed. program

You must complete coursework at the graduate level, and a faculty adviser and the college must approve the courses before they can be applied. Workshop credits are usually not accepted for transfer to C&I programs. Once your faculty adviser has approved the courses to be transferred in, please fill out the Academic Policy Petition. Under part 2 of this form, list:

  • Courses that are being transferred in (course number, title, credits and institution for each)
  • If applicable, U of MN course(es) they are substituting for (ex: RES 5000: Research in Education, 3 credits, Jones State University substituting for CI 5177: Practical Research)
  • If applicable, indicate if courses being transferred in will be used for the "electives" requirement (ex: RES 5000: Research in Education, 3 credits, Jones State University used to meet elective requirement)

Sign petition under "student certification" section on page 2 and send to your program adviser (see advisers below) for additional signatures and processing. Official transcripts are needed to transfer in courses. Please have official transcripts sent directly from the issuing institution to:

Registration

Are you an admitted student? You can register online for cousework.

Students who want to begin graduate coursework (M.Ed./M.A./Ph.D.) before admission must use the One Stop non-degree registration option. Please note: non-degree seeking students may only transfer in 12 credits into a graduate program if they choose to become fully enrolled. Students who choose to enroll in courses in non-degree seeking status are not eligible for federal financial aid and will need to pay for their course(s) by the tuition due date. The authorized signer for the Registration Request for Graduate Credit Form (for non-degree seeking students) in our department is Kristina Pearson (cigs@umn.edu; completed forms may be faxed to Kristina at 612-624-8277 for processing).

For questions, see FAQ for non-degree graduate registration.
