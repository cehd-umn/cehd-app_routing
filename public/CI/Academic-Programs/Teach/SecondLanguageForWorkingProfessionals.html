Second Language Education

Earn a license to teach ESL or world languages in Minnesota K-12 classrooms. Our license is highly transferable to other states and our candidates our highly sought-after by local school districts. This is a post-baccalaureate, part-time program, designed for practicing teachers of world languages (e.g., Arabic, Mandarin, and Spanish) and ESL (English as second language). To be an eligible candidate, you must be currently employed as a language teacher in a K-12 school setting. The program allows teachers to continue their employment while obtaining their initial teaching license, but does require 12 weeks of full time student teaching in a K-12 school.

Application priority deadlines:

  • July 1 for fall enrollment
  • November 1 for spring enrollment
  • December 15 for summer enrollment

Have a temporary license?
For practicing teachers

This program is designed for practicing teachers who have a temporary license and would like to earn a license while continuing to teach.

If you currently teach in an immersion program, or want to, this license is not for you. Instead, look at our Elementary education license. High-school immersion programs require a license in the subject you want to teach (e.g. math, science, or art).

Benefit from a graduate degree
License + M.Ed.

You may earn your licensure and master of education degree (M.Ed.) credits concurrently. Indicate your intent to enroll in the M.Ed. program when you apply.

To earn your M.Ed. complete 3 graduate-level credits on a topic approved by SLE faculty after your licensure credits are complete.

A graduate degree will earn you a pay increase and the additional course(s) will sharpen your skills and hone your confidence as an educator.

MTLE Basic Skills test
Prepare to apply

You are required by the Minnesota Board of Teaching to pass Minnesota Teacher Licensure Exams (MTLE). However, the ACT plus Writing, or SAT, exams may be used in lieu of the Basic Skills exam, if they were taken within the last ten years.

Email official test scores, or proof of registration, to the Office of Teacher Education (cehdlic@umn.edu) by the beginning of your first term.
