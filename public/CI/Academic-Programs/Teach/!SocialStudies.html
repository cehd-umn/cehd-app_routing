Overview

Our program is designed to foster a community of support. You will join a cohort of candidates who want to become social studies teachers.

You'll study together, learn from your field experiences together, support and be supported by a group of people with the same ambition as you: to become an amazing teacher.

Co-Teaching Mentorship

With ample opportunity to co-teach with a master teacher, you'll merge the theory of your coursework with the complex and compelling realities of the classroom.

Collaborating with and learning from experienced, inspired teachers, start your career with confidence.

Career Outlook

Candidates who earn a teaching license earn, on average, $6,000 more per year than a teacher with only a bachelor's degree.

The 2013-14 cohort had 93.8% first year job placement. Our graduates find teaching positions locally, nationally, and internationally.

Earn an M.Ed along with your licensure in just 13 months of full-time study.

A graduate degree will earn you a pay increase and make you a more competitive job candidate.

Requirements

A bachelor's degree and 2.80 GPA are required.

Favorite social studies teachers are masters of their subject, with significant completed coursework in one or more of the following areas: American studies | Anthropology | Applied Economics/Economics | Geography | History | Political Science | Psychology| Sociology

We awarded over $391,000 in grants and scholarships to teacher candidates last year. Apply for financial aid.

If you intend to teach a high-need subject in a public or private elementary or secondary school that serves low-income families, you may be eligible to receive grants of up to $4,000/year.TEACH grant information on Onestop.

If you teach full-time for five complete and consecutive years in schools that serve low-income families, you may be eligible for loan forgiveness of up to $17,500 through a program administered by the federal government.
