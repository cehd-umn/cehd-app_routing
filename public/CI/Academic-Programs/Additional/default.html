Additional Teaching License Programs
Individually customized courses of study

Our Additional licenses program are for licensed teachers who want to add a new field or scope to their existing Minnesota teaching license.

After you submit your application, faculty and advisers will recommend a customized program.

You maypursue an additional licensure while pursuing a master of education (M.Ed.) degree or later apply these credits toward a M.Ed degree.

We offer additional licensing in the following areas:

  • Art Education
  • Communication Arts
  • English as a Second Language
  • Reading
  • Math Education
  • Science Education
  • World Languages and Cultures
