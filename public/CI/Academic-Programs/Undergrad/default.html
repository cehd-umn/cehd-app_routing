Bachelor of Science in Elementary Education Foundations

The Bachelor of Science in Elementary Education Foundations program prepares you to work in collaboration with families and other education professionals to support the growth and development in elementary age children. Our program offers a mix theoretical groundwork and field-based experiences, providing opportunities to work in elementary schools with current elementary teachers and students.

  • Once completed, you are eligible (and receive preferential admission) to enter into a one year program where you earn a teaching license and a Master of Education degree.
  • You will be prepared to be an educator of all learners, with a flexible degree that translates well in other fields, such as government agencies, nonprofit organizations, learning centers and consulting firms.
  • This program is flexible encouraging you to study abroad, minor in subjects that fascinate you, and pursue activities that develop your leadership and character.  After all, students that take advantage of these opportunities make the best teachers.

Minor in English as a Second Language/TESL Certificate

The field of English language teaching is growing rapidly in this globalized world.  The ESL Minor/TESL Certificate will support you in a range of opportunities both domestically and abroad for teaching English language learners:

  • Work with both youth and adult learners of English
  • Teach English overseas
