Keep your active status!
Continuous Enrollment

Register every fall and spring to maintain active status.

If you don't, you will be discontinued and you will need to re-apply for admission and pay the application fee.

To receive your degree, you must be an active student in the term you file your paperwork and complete your coursework.

How-to
Avoid being discontinued

  • Register for GRAD 999, which is a no-credit, no-tuition option to keep your status active. You can only use it twice. Only use this option if you will return to complete your M.Ed. in a year or less.
  • If you aren't planning to finish your M.Ed. within a year, then submit a formal leave of absence request.
