Money for conferences
Travel Awards

Apply for travel awards at any time. Support is in the form of reimbursement after travel is complete. Funding also available from GradSEHD and COGS

Awards are granted to conference presenters, not participants. Recipients participate in the annual C+I Student Research Day.

Award maximums:

  • $400 - domestic travel
  • $600 - Canada | Hawaii
  • $800 - international travel
  • $3,000 - total while in program
  • One conference per year (Sept.-Aug.)
