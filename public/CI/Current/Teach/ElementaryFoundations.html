Liberal education requirements

Major requirements

Registration tips:

  • Be sure to register for course sections that specify they are for elementary education students, not ones for other license areas
  • You need to take your introductory block (CI 3211 + 3212 + either EPSY 3119 or 5001) your first semester in the major -- consider doing it in France! Bonne idée!
  • Take Survey of art activities (CI 3001) after you complete your introductory block
  • Take Intro to child psychology (CPSY 2301) before or concurrently with the special education block
  • Take the special education block (EPSY 5613 + 5616 + CI 3283) after the introductory block and before the reading block; take all the special ed. block courses the same semester
  • Complete Diversity in children's literature (CI 3401) before you take the reading block
  • Take your Linguistics course (CI 3610 OR LING 3001 OR ENGL 3601) before or concurrently with the reading block; CI 3610 is preferred
  • Take your reading block (CI 5413 + 5414) courses together
  • All major coursework must be taken A/F when available, and you must earn a C- or better as well as maintain a minimum 2.8 GPA each semester
  • Your degree requirements must be complete by the end of spring term prior to starting the 5th year license program in the fall

You're almost there!
Undergraduate Graduation

  1. Stay on track to satisfy all requirements. Review your APAS prior to and after registration each semester. Contact your adviser if your APAS report is incorrect.
  2. Before your final semester, meet with your adviser. Make sure your APAS report is correct, as this is what we use to clear your degree so you can graduate. Degrees are cleared after final grades are posted.
  3. Review the CE+HD Graduate & Commencement website---it's where you'll find links to the Application for Undergraduate Degree form, submission deadline information, and commencement ceremony details.

Benefit from a graduate degree
Get licensed + Finish your M.Ed.

Your senior year, apply to the licensure program. In just two more semesters you'll earn your Minnesota K - 6 teaching license and your Master of Education (M.Ed. degree). Start in August and finish by early June.
