Apply for licensure + Finish your M.Ed.

Your license and your M.Ed. are granted separately and require
two different completion processes.

Apply for licensure

You may earn your teaching license and master of education credits concurrently.

To earn your M.Ed. complete 3 graduate-level credits on a topic approved by SLE faculty after your licensure credits are complete.

You must maintain a 2.80 overall GPA.

If you plan to take a semester off, see info on maintaining continuous enrollment.

When you’re ready to graduate, follow these steps:
Step #1: Submit Program Plan Form to your faculty adviser for approval

  • Complete your Program Plan Form and submit to your advisor in the first month of your last semester of coursework
  • Submit all requests for transfer credit to your advisor as early as possible


Step #2: Complete the Application for Degree in MyU

  • Complete the application before the 1st of the month you wish to graduate—see Application for Degree website for instructions
  • MyU>Academics tab>Degree Progress>Click on "Apply to Graduate" link
