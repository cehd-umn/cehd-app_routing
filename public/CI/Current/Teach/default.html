Degree Completion and Licensure

  • Students must maintain a 2.80 overall GPA and maintain continuous enrollment.

    In addition to completing all program requirements, refer to the graduation checklist and timeline for instructions on how to apply for your degree. As you prepare to graduate, fill out the forms in your program's degree completion packet.

    CEHD Graduate Student Operations (cehdgrad@umn.edu) clears degrees for graduation on a monthly basis. The degree completion packet must be completed on or before the first day of the month that the student intends to graduate.

    Once the degree is noted on the University transcript, the student has technically graduated. The college holds one commencement ceremony each year in May to recognize all students in the college who have graduated within that academic year.

    Apply by March 1 if you plan to participate in the college commencement ceremony in May.
