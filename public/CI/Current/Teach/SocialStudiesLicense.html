Checklist + MTLE
Newly admitted students

Checklist for newly admitted students. Complete checklist items as soon as possible.

You are required by the Minnesota Board of Teaching to pass Minnesota Teacher Licensure Exams (MTLE).

  1. Basic skills. The ACT plus Writing, or SAT, exams may be used in lieu of the Basic Skills exam, if they were taken within the last ten years. Email official test scores, or proof of registration, to the Office of Teacher Education (cehdlic@umn.edu) by the beginning of your first term. Essential Academic Skills: information + registration
  2. Pedagogy. Take the pedagogy exam during your student teaching practicum.
  3. Content. Take the social studies content exam as soon as possible.

Apply for Licensure and Finish your M.Ed.

Your license and your M.Ed. are granted separately and require
two different completion processes. Apply for your license when you have completed your coursework.
You must maintain a 2.80 GPA.
Apply for licensure

If you’ve already completed your coursework and the term has ended, you’ll need to register for GRAD 999 in order to keep your enrollment active as the University can only clear a degree in an active student status.  Use GRAD 999 also if you plan to take a semester off. Visit the Continuous Enrollment webpage for more information.

When you’re ready to graduate, follow these steps:
Step #1: Submit Program Plan Form to your faculty adviser for approval

  • Complete your program plan form and submit to your advisor in the first month of your last semester of coursework
  • Submit all requests for transfer credit to your advisor as early as possible

 
Step #2: Complete the Application for Degree in MyU

  • Complete the application before the 1st of the month you wish to graduate—see Application for Degree website for instructions
  • MyU>Academics tab>Degree Progress>Click on "Apply to Graduate" link
