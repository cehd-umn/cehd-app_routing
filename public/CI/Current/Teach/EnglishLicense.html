MTLE
Basic skills exam

You are required by the Minnesota Board of Teaching to pass Minnesota Teacher Licensure Exams (MTLE). However, the ACT plus Writing, or SAT, exams may be used in lieu of the Basic Skills exam, if they were taken within the last ten years.

Email official test scores, or proof of registration, to the Office of Teacher Education (cehdlic@umn.edu) by the beginning of your first term.

Career services

Finish your M.Ed.

To earn your M.Ed., you will teach for a year then complete 12 graduate-level credits. The credits can be completed on a part-time basis or during the summer. While teaching, take a leave of absence or utilize GRAD 999. See the Continuous Enrollment webpage for more information.

You must be an active student in the term you file your paperwork to graduate. Use GRAD 999 to maintain active status if you degree will be cleared after your last semester of coursework has ended. GRAD 999 can only be used a maximum of two times and should only be used in Fall and Spring semesters; you are not required to maintain active status in Summer.

You must maintain a 2.80 GPA.

When you’re ready to graduate, follow these steps:
Step #1: Submit Program Plan Form to your faculty adviser for approval

  • Complete your Program Plan Form and submit to your advisor in the first month of your last semester of coursework
  • Submit all requests for transfer credit to your advisor as early as possible

 
Step #2: Complete the Application for Degree in MyU

  • Complete the application before the 1st of the month you wish to graduate—see Application for Degree website for instructions
  • MyU>Academics tab>Degree Progress>Click on "Apply to Graduate" link
