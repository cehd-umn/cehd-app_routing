Transfer credits

Transfer a maximum of 12 graduate semester credits, including any combination of the following coursework:

  • Graduate semester credits from fully accredited institutions other than the University of Minnesota
  • Graduate credits completed at the University of Minnesota before admission to the M.Ed. program

Both faculty adviser and college approval are required. Workshop credits are usually not accepted.

After your faculty adviser approves the transfer courses, please fill out the Academic Policy Petition. Under part 2 of this form, list:

  • Transfer courses (course number, title, credits and institution for each)
  • If applicable, the U of MN course(es) they are substituting for (ex: RES 5000: Research in Education, 3 credits, Jones State University substituting for CI 5177: Practical Research)
  • If applicable, indicate if transfer courses will be used for the "electives" requirement (ex: RES 5000: Research in Education, 3 credits, Jones State University used to meet elective requirement)

Sign petition under "student certification" section on page 2 and send to your program adviser for additional signatures and processing.

How to Complete Your Degree

Submit Program Plan Form to your faculty adviser for approval

  • Program Plan Forms can be found above for each M.Ed in Professional Studies concentration and should be submitted in the first month of your last semester of coursework
  • Submit all requests for transfer credit to your advisor as early as possible
Complete the Application for Degree in MyU before the first of the month you wish to graduate in. Navigate to the application via MyU through the “Academics” tab > “Degree Progress” and click on the Apply to Graduate link.

Important! Maintaining continuous enrollment: You must be an active student in the term you file your paperwork to graduate. Use GRAD 999 to maintain active status if you degree will be cleared after your last semester of coursework has ended. GRAD 999 can only be used a maximum of two times and should only be used in Fall and Spring semesters; you are not required to maintain active status in Summer. Find more information about continuous enrollment.

Note: Directions on the Graduate School’s website for M.Ed. degree completion refer to submitting a GPAS Planner however Curriculum and Instruction does not use that tool. The process outlined above is what you should refer to when planning to graduate.
