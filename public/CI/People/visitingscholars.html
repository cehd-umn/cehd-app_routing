Information for
Visiting Scholars

In the spirit of collegiality and to foster the further development of knowledge, we host a limited number of scholars who wish to conduct research in collaboration with our faculty.

Benefits include:

  • reading and borrowing privileges in the University Libraries
  • sitting in on courses and seminars (with instructor approval)
  • participating in the social community of the department
  • shared office space with access to a computer and printer (if available)
  • access to computer labs and copying privileges
  • services of the International Scholar and Student Services Office

Interested?
How to apply

We give preference to Ph.D.-holding scholars and researchers. We occasionally grant permission to advanced graduate students from other institutions pursuing dissertation research.

First identify an academic contact within the department whose work relates to your research interest. Second, write a letter of inquiry to that faculty member. Please only write to one faculty member. This letter should:

  1. outline the proposed research,
  2. explain why the department or particular faculty member's work is the best fit for applicant's proposed research,
  3. state requested period of visit, and
  4. include a current curriculum vitae.

Our department chair will send you a letter of invitation after the application has been received, reviewed and approved. This process may take 2-3 weeks.

Other requirements
After we invite you to come

  • a letter indicating home institution approval of leave
  • a department faculty host who agrees to provide guidance on a chosen research project.
  • full funding with financial resources outside the University of Minnesota, and financial responsibility for visa processing fees of the University of Minnesota's International Student and Scholar Services.
  • health insurance individually, and for any accompanying family members for the duration of the agreed upon time period (options available through University of Minnesota).
