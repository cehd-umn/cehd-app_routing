Elementary Education Foundations

Bachelor of Science

Elementary age children take giant leaps in learning and development throughout an academic year. Elementary teachers take on the responsibility of ushering their students to the next milestones. For young children, each day is almost overwhelming with new possibilities. In order to ensure their students reach success, elementary educators need to be able to capture and hold children's attention, adapt easily to changing circumstances and teach a variety of subjects to all kinds of learners. Additionally, elementary educators assess student levels and progress, and interact with families to ensure their students’ educational needs are being met.

The University of Minnesota offers a Bachelor of Science degree in Elementary Education Foundations that offers a range of possible career avenues for its graduates. Once completed, most students enter into a fifth year of additional coursework to earn a teaching license and a master of education degree. If Elementary Education Foundations students would like to go on to earn their license to be a teacher, they receive preferential admission to the Initial Licensure Program at the University of Minnesota.

Points of Pride

  • Offers structured path to the initial teaching license and Master of Education program.
  • Incorporates clinical experiences in real elementary classrooms.
  • Prepares teacher candidates to work with elementary aged children in a broad range of environments.
  • Develops teacher candidates’ cultural competencies so that upon graduation, candidates are experienced in supporting the learning of ALL students.

Your Experience

The Elementary Education Foundations program is a stand-alone bachelor’s degree that is carefully crafted to flow smoothly into a one year, combined initial teaching license and master of education program. That means within 5 years, you will have earned a bachelor’s degree, a license to teach Elementary Education in Minnesota, and a master’s degree. Throughout the Foundations program, you will have clinical experiences, such as observing elementary teachers in their classrooms, working one-on-one with students, and creating your own lesson plans. These experiences build confidence so that by the time you finish the Foundations program, you will feel certain about your career path.

If you want to go onto be a teacher, you will know the grade level you want to teach and the school setting you’d most enjoy. For the fifth year, M.Ed./I.L.P., our advisers will place you with a cooperating teacher for a full school year. In the first semester, you will alternate between weeks in the classroom co-teaching, and weeks in courses discussing your experiences and observations with your peers. In the second semester, you will be in working side-by-side with your co-teacher.

To learn more about the 5th year initial license and Master of Education program, please visit the M.Ed./ILP page.

Career options

Our program is specifically designed to prepare you to be an exceptional educator. Many Elementary Education students seamlessly transition into the Elementary Education Master of Education/Initial Licensure Program after completing the Foundations program. Once they obtain their initial license, they typically go on to become teachers. However, you will find that the skills you obtain in our program prepare you for other career paths as well. For example, you might also consider careers in:

  • adoption agencies
  • boards of education
  • consulting firms
  • day care centers
  • educational publishers
  • learning centers
  • nonprofit organizations
  • test-preparation companies

Please visit our Career Services section for additional information on careers and employers in Elementary Education.
