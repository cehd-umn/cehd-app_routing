Congratulations on your recent implementation of the Firefly template. Created on April 2nd, 2010, Firefly is lightweight and easy to implement. The Firefly template merges core.css and the design.css together into one easy to update style sheet. If you so chose, Firefly also accepts core components such as jQuery, AJAX, clam shell menus and even accordion menus. Read on for implementation tips.
