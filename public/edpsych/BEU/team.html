Beyond Evaluation Use Team

Beyond Evaluation Use Team: Boris Volkov, Stacie Toal, Frances Lawrenz, Lija Greenseid, Kelli Johnson, Jean King
Not pictured - Denise Roseland, Gina Johnson

Primary Investigators

Dr. Jean King

Jean King is a professor in the Department of Educational Policy and Administration at the University of Minnesota where she serves as Coordinator of the Evaluation Studies Program.  She holds an A.B., M.S., and Ph.D. in from Cornell University.  In 1995 her professional evaluation activities using participatory methods resulted in the Myrdal Award for Evaluation Practice from the American Evaluation Association, and in 1999 she was awarded the Association’s Ingle Award for Extraordinary Service.

Dr. Frances Lawrenz

Frances Lawrenz is a professor in the Department of Educational Psychology and Associate Vice President for Research at the University of Minnesota.  Her major research focus is science and mathematics program evaluation. Her evaluations utilize a variety of techniques to best fit the needs of a given situation and usually involve mixing methods in a variety of ways. 

Graduate Research Assistants

Lija Greenseid

Lija Greenseid is a doctoral student in the Quantitative Methods in Education program in the Department of Educational Psychology at the University of Minnesota.  Her research interests are the empirical study of evaluation use and evaluation theory and history.  Prior to working on her doctorate, Lija worked with non-profit organizations in the US, UK, and Israel and traveled extensively.

 

Gina Johnson

Gina Johnson is a doctoral student in the Quantitative Methods in Education program in the Department of Educational Psychology at the University of Minnesota. Prior to her doctoral studies, Gina spent time as a special education teacher, an instructor of environmental science and faculty development coordinator at a small college, and as a data analyst researching issues impacting higher education in the Midwest. Gina's research interests include assessment of student learning outcomes at the higher education level and teacher preparation and professional development.

 

Kelli Johnson

Kelli Johnson is a doctoral candidate in Evaluation Studies in Educational Policy and Administration and a research fellow in Health Policy and Management at the University of Minnesota. She has 15 years of experience in public policy at the legislative and state agency levels. Her experience in conducting evaluations has been primarily in social services, health care, and health professions education.

Denise Roseland

Denise Roseland is a doctoral student in Evaluation Studies in Educational Policy and Administration. She has 8 years experience as a public school teacher and state education agency administrator prior to beginning her doctoral studies. Her research interests are in evaluation of science, technology, engineering and math (STEM) education programs and in gender differences in STEM education and STEM career aspirations and attainment.

Stacie Toal

Dr. Stacie Toal recently completed her Ph.D. in Educational Policy and Administration, Evaluation Studies. She has spent the majority of her career working in higher education, helping colleges and universities answer questions related to enrollment management and institutional effectiveness. Stacie's research interests are evaluation involvement and use.

Boris Volkov

Dr. Boris Volkov recently completed his Ph.D. Evaluation Studies program in the Department of Educational Policy and Administration at the University of Minnesota. His interests include evaluation capacity building in organizations, the evaluation of educational technology and science projects, and human resource development in organizations.
