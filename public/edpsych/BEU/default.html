Welcome to the Beyond Evaluation Use project

Beyond Evaluation Use is a three-year research project, funded by the National Science Foundation (NSF), conducted by a team of researchers at the University of Minnesota.  The project is studying the use and influence of evaluations of four NSF-funded programs by examining the relationship between the extent of involvement of evaluation stakeholders and the long-term impact of the evaluations on project staff, the science, technology, engineering, and mathematics community, and the evaluation community.

The purposes of the project are fourfold:

  1. To contrast different types of evaluation use and influence;
  2. To increase knowledge of the use/influence of evaluation activities in large scale national programs;
  3. To propose models for extending the influence of evaluation findings;
  4. To disseminate knowledge about the use/influence of evaluation activities in large scale national programs.
