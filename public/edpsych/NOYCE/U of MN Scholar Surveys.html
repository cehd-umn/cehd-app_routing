U of MN Scholar Surveys:

Brief Description

 

As part of the evaluation, web-based surveys were developed for the scholars.  These included several different versions so that the items would be applicable to scholars in different stages of their careers (e.g., graduated and teaching, still enrolled as a student, etc.).

Below are 5 versions of the scholar surveys used for the evaluation.   The Full Versions are the actual ones the scholars had filled out. The Short Versions are the U of MN Noyce Evaluation Team revised condensed forms of the surveys based on psychological and statistical examinations.  The final survey listed contains the items that all scholars who responded to the surveys had filled out.
