Join our lab

If you are an undergrad or person from the community and would like to join our lab, please fill out our RA form.

If you are a graduate student and would like to join our lab, please fill out our GRA form.

Requirements for joining the lab:

  • RA requirements
    • Attending monthly IGDILab project meetings
    • Participating in data-collection, data-analysis or interpretation
    • Semi-annual review of performance based on identified goals for their participation in IGDI work
  • GRA requirements
    • If you are a GRA on appointment with the IGDILab or are a GRA with separate funding who would like to volunteer hours to the IGDILab we require you:
    • Attending weekly IGDILab project meetings
    • Participating in data- collection, data-analysis or interpretation
    • Disseminate findings in local, state, and national venues
    • Participate in a semi-annual review of performance based on identified goals for your contributions to the Lab

Note: If you are a GRA funded by the IGDILab you will have unique requirements based on your specific project. Your PI will inform you of your roles and responsibilities as part of that position.
