Participating Schools

The CBAS-R project is continuing its effort to recruit new schools for participation.  Schools who participate receive money that will go towards their school’s library.  Participation in this project gives schools a chance to be a part of research which will benefit children for years to come.  CBAS-R gives students extra practice in reading, as they are engaging in reading activities during the test session. Also, once the project goes online, schools who participated will be able to use the assessment for free.
