Technology-Based Early Language Comprehension Intervention

Panayiota Kendeou, Kristen McMaster, Theodore Christ, and Mary Jane White

The purpose of this Development and Innovation project is to develop a Technology-Based Early Language Comprehension Intervention (TELCI) to improve reading comprehension of students with or at risk for disabilities who experience comprehension difficulties in the early elementary years (grades one and two).
