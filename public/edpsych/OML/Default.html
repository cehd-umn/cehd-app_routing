This is the home page of the Observational Methods Lab (OML) in the Department of Educational Psychology at the University of Minnesota. We are located in the basement of the Education Sciences Building, room 150.

The OML offers a variety of resources and services for students and faculty whose research projects utilize direct observational data and computer assisted real time data.

For more information on what the lab is about, our available resources, and how to contact us, please follow the links on the menu.

Lab Users: Equipment manuals, information, and guidelines on checking out equipment are available on the resources page.
