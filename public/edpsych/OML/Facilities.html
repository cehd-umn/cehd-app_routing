Facilities

Where is the OML?
The OML is composed of three rooms (146, 148, 150) in the Education Sciences Building at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities campus.

What kind of equipment does the OML have?
It contains a variety of equipment to assist in observational data collection as well as computers and software for performing analyses. Please see the Resources page for a comprehensive list of software, hardware, and services.

How can I gain access to this equipment?
To find out more about checking out equipment, contact the OML coordinator.

Can I receive training on the software or equipment?
Yes, contact the OML to schedule training.

Will the _____ program work for my research?
The OML can assist you in determining whether or not a certain program is best for your research. Contact us or check out our Related Links or Current Projects pages to see what other people are doing with our equipment.
