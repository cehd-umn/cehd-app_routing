Getting Started

Study Expectations

  • All teachers who enroll in our study are expected to participate for two years (see timeline of participation) AND will be  randomly assigned  to either the Cohort 1 (treatment) or Cohort 2 (control) group. Cohort 1 teachers will teach ratio/proportion, and percent topics using SBI materials for two consecutive years, whereas cohort 2 teachers will teach the same topics using their district mandated textbook the first year and use SBI materials the second year.  For each teacher, only one typical seventh-grade class of studentshttp://drydock.cehd.umn.edu/EdPsych/MARS/Getting-Started.html will be randomly selected to participate in the study. However, treatment teachers using SBI materials are allowed to use them with all of their typical seventh-grade math classes.

Timeline of Participation

  Cohort 1 seventh-grade math teachers Cohort 2 seventh-grade math teachers

Year 1

(2015-2016)

 Attend two-day professional development (PD) training sessions (date and location TBD); teach ratio/proportion, and percent topics using SBI materials. *Note that instruction will align with district's pacing guide and be taught at the same time as Cohort 2 teachers teach the same content. Teach ratio/proportion, and percent topics using district assigned textbook.

Year 2

(2016-2017)

 No PD; teach ratio/proportion, and percent topics using SBI materials. Attend two-day PD training sessions (date and location TBD); teach ratio/proportion, and percent topics using SBI materials.
  • All participating teachers will work with project staff to complete a test of their knowledge of proportional reasoning prior to the start of the study.
  • All teachers will administer two mathematics assessments (proportional problem-solving and overall mathematical problem solving) at the beginning and end of the study and one assessment (proportional problem solving) at the end of the school year.
  • Cohort 1 teachers in Years 1 and 2 and Cohort 2 teachers in Year 2 will administer a brief student questionnaire to assess their acceptability of the SBI intervention.
  • Cohort 1 teachers in Years 1 and 2 and Cohort 2 teachers in Year 2 will complete a questionnaire to assess their acceptability of the SBI intervention as well as its perceived effectiveness.
  • All teachers will allow project videographer to conduct one video recording of classroom instruction on ratio, proportion, or percent.

Benefits of Participating in the Study:

  • Learn a new method (SBI, an evidence-based instructional approach) for teaching proportional reasoning
  • Improve your seventh-grade students’ learning of ratio, proportion, and percent
  • Collect generous financial stipends each year for participating in the study
  • Receive all intervention materials for you and your students (Cohort 2 teachers will receive the same materials only in Year 2)
  • Obtain reports of the study findings for your individual classroom (our research team will score all assessments and also provide the school principal/district with a copy of the study findings by classrooms/schools)
