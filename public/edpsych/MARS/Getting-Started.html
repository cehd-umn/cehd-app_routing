Getting Started

Study Expectations

  • All teachers who enroll in our study are expected to participate for two years (see timeline of participation) AND will be randomly assigned  to either the Cohort 1 (Treatment) or Cohort 2 (Control) group. Treatment teachers will teach ratio/proportion, and percent topics using SBI materials for two consecutive years, whereas Control teachers will teach the same topics using their district mandated textbook the first year and use SBI materials the second year.  For each teacher, only one typical seventh-grade class of students will be randomly selected to participate in the study. However, treatment teachers using SBI materials are allowed to use them with all of their typical seventh-grade math classes.

Requirements of Participating in the Study:

  • All participating teachers will work with project staff to complete a test of their knowledge of proportional reasoning prior to the start of the study.
  • All teachers will administer two mathematics assessments (proportional problem-solving and overall mathematical problem solving) at the beginning and end of the study and one assessment (proportional problem solving) at the end of the school year.
  • Treatment teachers in Years 1 and 2 and Control teachers in Year 2 will administer a brief student questionnaire to assess their acceptability of the SBI intervention.
  • All teachers will allow project videographer to conduct one video recording of classroom instruction on ratio, proportion, or percent.

 

Benefits of Participating in the Study:

  • Learn a new method (SBI, an evidence-based instructional approach) for teaching proportional reasoning
  • Improve your seventh-grade students’ learning of ratio, proportion, and percent
  • Collect generous financial stipends each year for participating in the study
  • Receive all intervention materials for you and your students (Control teachers will receive the same materials only in Year 2)
  • Obtain reports of the study findings for your individual classroom (our research team will score all assessments and also provide the school principal/district with a copy of the study findings by classrooms/schools)
