PROJECT MARS (Mathematical Reasoning Strategies)

Dr. Asha Jitendra, Rodney Wallace Professor for the Advancement of Teaching and Learning, in the Department of Educational Psychology at the University of Minnesota is the Principle Investigator (PI) of the project. 

The purpose of the four-year project entitled, "Learning of Ratio and Proportion Problem-Solving Using Schema-Based Instruction: Efficacy and Sustainability," is to evaluate the efficacy of an intervention (schema-based instruction, SBI) aimed at improving student learning of ratio and proportion. Our primary research objective is to assess the immediate and long-term impact of SBI on students’ mathematics problem solving and proportional reasoning. Secondary objectives include assessing the immediate and long-term impact of SBI with seventh grade students when implemented by SBI-experienced teachers, implementation factors that are associated with effectiveness, SBI and teacher-classroom characteristics that moderate the impact of student-level characteristics on mathematical problem solving and proportional reasoning, and effects on students’ metacognitive strategy knowledge as well as effects on student acceptability of SBI.
