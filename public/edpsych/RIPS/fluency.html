Fluency

The intervention guides and materials below are targeted to meet student needs in the area of reading fluency.

Listening Passage Preview/Phrase Drill

Objective: Students will increase word recognition accuracy when reading sentences in story passages using listening passage preview (LPP) and phrase drill (PD) strategies.
Skill(s): Accuracy in word recognition, reading fluency
Intervention Need: Accuracy (achieve 90-95% accuracy when reading words in sentences/connected text).
Intervention Guide: Listening Passage Preview/Phrase Drill
Intervention Materials:
  • Instructional Reading Passages (Grade 1, Grade 2-3)
  • Partner Reading Rules
  • Partner Word Correction Guide

Repeated Reading with Partners

Objective: Students will increase reading rate (automaticity) when reading sentences and story passages using repeated reading (RR) strategies with peer support.
Skill(s): Reading fluency
Intervention Need: Automaticity (achieve 95% accuracy with automatic responses in reading connected text).
Intervention Guide: Repeated Reading with Partners
Intervention Materials:
  • Instructional Reading Passages (Grade 1, Grade 2-3)
  • Partner Reading Rules
  • Partner Word Correction Guide
  • Repeated Reading Log
