Using Curriculum-Based Measurement to Set Goals and Make Data-Based Decisions

In this module we talk about the purpose and guidelines of Curriculum-Based Measurement–Reading (CBM-R).

After reviewing the materials in this module, you should be able to:

  • Describe the purpose of CBM-R.
  • Describe the research supporting CBM-R.
  • Administer, score, and graph CBM-R data.
  • Use CBM-R to monitor student progress in reading.
