Vocabulary

The intervention guides and materials below are targeted to meet student needs in the area of vocabulary.

Sight Word Flashcards

Objective: Students in grades K-3 will build accuracy and master a set of sight words using an incremental rehearsal flashcard approach.
Skill(s): Sight words
Intervention Need: Sight Word Flashcards
Intervention Guide: Sight Word Flashcards (kindergarten, first grade, second-third grade)
Intervention Materials:
  • Kindergarten 50 Sight Word Cards (small, medium, large)
  • First Grade 150 Sight Word Cards (small, medium, large)
  • Second-Third Grade 300 Sight Word Cards (small, medium, large)
  • Sight Word Phrases (50 kindergarten, 150 first grade, 300 second-third grade)
  • Sight Word Lesson Log (kindergarten, first grade, second-third grade)
  • Second-Third Grade 300 Sight Word Informal Assessment

Sight Word Bingo

Objective: Students will build automaticity and retention of a set of sight words using a version of “bingo” to practice the words.
Skill(s): Sight Words
Intervention Need: Automaticity and retention (achieve 95% accuracty with automatic responses in reading the sight words)
Intervention Guide: Sight Word Bingo
Intervention Materials:
  • Small sight word flashcards (kindergarten, first grade, second-third grade)
  • Bingo Template Card (3X3, 4X4, 5X5)
  • Sight Word Phrases (50 kindergarten, 150 first grade, 300 second-third grade)
  • Sight Word Lesson Log (kindergarten, first grade, second-third grade)
